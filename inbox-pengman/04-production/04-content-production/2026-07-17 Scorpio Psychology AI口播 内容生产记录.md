### 纯文本脚本（供 AI 配音）

```
The biggest problem with Scorpio is they'll leave before you get the chance to.

And most people think that's cruelty. That they just don't care enough to stay.

But for people with strong Scorpio placements, it tends to be the opposite. The more they care, the harder it becomes to stay open.

There's a pattern here. When closeness builds, something pulls back. Walk away first. Control the ending. Don't let anyone get close enough to leave you.

It looks like silence. It looks like walls. But underneath that — it's often a reflex around trust. Not cruelty. Protection.

In astrology, Pluto rules Scorpio. And Pluto is about extremes — total trust or total shutdown. Vulnerability or control. Transformation or destruction. There's rarely an in-between.

So when someone with heavy Scorpio energy goes quiet, it may not be indifference. It may be that staying feels more dangerous than leaving.

This isn't everyone with a Scorpio Sun. It depends on your Pluto, your Moon, your whole chart. But if you recognize this pattern — now you have a different lens for it.
```

### 第二版设计说明

- **Hook（0-3s）**：强断言痛点，对标 @terryhales1 "The biggest problem with [sign]"——直接砸判断停住手指。Pengman 从四个选项中选择此版。
- **反转（3-8s）**：快速翻转 "most people think that's cruelty"。
- **占星象征（8-42s）**：Pluto 只作为 trust / control / vulnerability / transformation 的象征，不断言神经系统因果、依恋类型或早年创伤。
- **范围限定**：使用 "people with strong Scorpio placements" / "someone with heavy Scorpio energy" / "not everyone with a Scorpio Sun"，不把所有 Scorpio Sun 写成同一种人格。
- **语气**：使用 "tends to" / "often" / "may" 代替确定性判断。
- **收束（50-58s）**："depends on your Pluto, your Moon, your whole chart" + "different lens"，开放式，无 CTA。
- **时长**：约 55-58 秒。

# Scorpio × Cancer 沉默博弈 AI 口播 — 统一 Brief 与脚本

## Brief 摘要

- **Stage 3 编号**：首个配对内容试探。
- **目标账号**：② AI 占星师人设 / `@miraaastrology`。
- **目标用户**：18–35 岁；具有 strong Scorpio 或 Cancer placements 的自我代入者；经历过关系中"双方都沉默等对方先开口"的用户。
- **内容目标**：作为 miraaastrology 首个星座配对内容，验证"双星座视角 + 具体关系行为"能否同时激活两个星座受众。
- **用户问题**：为什么 Scorpio 和 Cancer 在一起时，会陷入双方都不先开口的沉默僵局？
- **内容承诺**：分别解释两个水象星座的沉默背后各自不同的心理需求，不判断对错。
- **核心洞察**：Cancer 的沉默是在测试"你会不被要求就来找我吗"；Scorpio 的沉默是在确认"你会在我什么都不给的时候主动靠近吗"。同一个行为，不同的内心逻辑，相同的僵局结果。
- **Hook**：`Scorpio and Cancer will both go silent — not because they stopped caring, but because they're both waiting to see who breaks first.`
- **CTA**：本轮继续不放口播 CTA。
- **约束**：不把沉默病理化；不做诊断；不绝对化；保持观察者姿态；目标时长 55–60 秒。

## 为什么做配对内容

- 战略报告阶段三明确提出 Scorpio 关系组合方向，"Scorpio × Cancer: 两个都不说，但都在等对方先开口"已被列为候选。
- 配对内容同时服务两个星座受众，理论上双倍触达。
- 多源占星数据验证：Cancer-Scorpio 沉默循环是该配对最高频出现的关系行为模式（dxpnet 4885 replies 中 recurring cycles of distance and return 为核心主题）。
- 本条保持 miraa 已验证的断言式行为 Hook 形式，只增加"双视角"一个新变量。

### 纯文本配音稿

```text
Scorpio and Cancer will both go silent — not because they stopped caring, but because they're both waiting to see who breaks first.

And it's not a fight. There's no argument. Something just shifted. A tone. A delay. A response that felt a little too short. And suddenly they're both watching instead of talking.

Cancer pulls back because they need you to come closer without being asked. That's how they know it's real. If they have to tell you they're hurting, it doesn't count the same way.

Scorpio pulls back because they're running a test they'll never admit to. They need to see if you'll reach for them when they give you nothing. Silence isn't punishment. It's surveillance.

So now you have two people who both feel everything — both sitting in the same silence — both convinced the other one should go first. And every hour that passes becomes evidence. Cancer thinks: they don't care enough. Scorpio thinks: they're not safe enough.

They're not ignoring each other. They're both screaming on the inside and calling it patience.
```

2. Hook 首帧完整可读；`who breaks first` 使用唯一强调色。
3. 口播语气保持克制、平静的洞察者感觉 — 不嘲讽也不心疼。
4. Scorpio glyph 和 Cancer glyph 可同时微亮出现（26-38s 段），不喧宾夺主。
5. 本条是首个配对内容，观察两个星座受众的反应差异：哪个星座评论更多、是否出现"站队"现象。

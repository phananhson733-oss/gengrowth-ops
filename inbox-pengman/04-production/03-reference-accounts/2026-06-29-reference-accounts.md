## TikTok

- https://www.tiktok.com/@maxcartexofficial
  - AI podcast / talking-head format. Uses a fixed male podcast-style avatar, large captions, and high-emotion hooks around psychology, women, behavior, and zodiac signs.
  - Reference value: strong listicle hooks such as `Five things Leo hates` and `The most dangerous zodiac sign...`; useful for testing identity-based astrology hooks.
  - AstrologyWiki fit: borrow the fixed AI host + short list structure, but soften overly absolute claims and connect each video back to Sun / Moon / Rising / Venus / houses.

  - Reference value: more brand-safe than pure shock hooks; `The Psychology of a Leo Personality` style framing is close to AstrologyWiki's desired `astrology explained through psychology` direction.
  - AstrologyWiki fit: best model for a credible AI podcast format. Use titles like `The Psychology of Leo Moon` or `Why Scorpio Rising feels hard to read`.

  - AstrologyWiki fit: borrow the elder-host authority and emotional pain-point framing, but avoid heavy profanity and unsupported sign rankings.

## X

- https://x.com/satyastrology
  - Traditional astrology reference account with clear positioning around `clarity & depth`, real transits, and trust-building interpretation. The account links back to satyastrology.com and uses X as both community surface and conversion entry.
  - Reference value: high-frequency transit posts, short practical insights, simple visuals, and open-ended questions. Strong example formula: `Jupiter is coming to Leo tomorrow` + `Which house is Leo in your birth chart?`
  - Operating pattern: value first, then conversion. Free daily/weekly transit insight and educational replies build trust, while website links support reports, courses, articles, or deeper services.
  - Community pattern: not one-way posting. The useful loop is `timely transit post -> personal question -> user replies -> professional follow-up`, with longer replies demonstrating authority.
  - AstrologyWiki fit: adapt the format without copying the traditional-astrology positioning. Use `psychological astrology grounded in real astronomy` as the differentiator, and pair transit questions with free tools and Wiki pages.
  - Immediate template: `Today's key transit: [event]. [1-2 sentence psychological insight]. Which house/sign/placement does this activate in your chart? Generate your chart here: [tool link]`.
  - Suggested content mix: 40% timely transits + interaction questions, 30% free tool promotion, 20% Wiki article teasers, 10% brand/other.
  - Watchout: do not overuse technical traditional terms such as dignity, almuten, or house-system debates unless they directly help the user. AstrologyWiki should stay practical, psychological, and tool-driven.

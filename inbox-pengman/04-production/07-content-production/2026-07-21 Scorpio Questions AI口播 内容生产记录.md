# Scorpio Psychology AI 口播第四条 — 统一 Brief 与脚本

## Brief 摘要

- **Stage 1 编号**：C4。
- **目标账号**：② AI 占星师人设 / `@miraaastrology`。
- **目标用户**：18–35 岁女性为主；具有 strong Scorpio placements 的自我代入者，以及感觉被 Scorpio 用”明知故问”试探的关系受众。
- **内容目标**：完成第四个 Scorpio 延续样本，不重复前三条的 withdrawal、testing behavior 和 access/boundary；把变量收窄为”已经知道答案，仍询问以核对诚实”。
- **用户问题**：为什么 Scorpio 会问一个明显已经知道答案的问题？
- **内容承诺**：解释这种行为背后的一致性验证逻辑，而非包装成读心或操控。
- **核心洞察**：对 strong Scorpio placements 来说，提问不一定是为了获取信息，而是为了确认对方是否会如实回答——不一致本身就是信号。
- **Hook**：`Scorpio will ask you a question they already know the answer to. They're not looking for information. They're checking whether you'll tell the truth.`
- **CTA**：本轮继续不放口播 CTA。

## 去重与实验控制

| 条目 | 核心机制 | 本条是否重复 |
|---|---|---|
| 第 1 条 | withdrawal / leave first | 否；本条没有离开动作 |
| 第 2 条 | testing / watch your reaction | 近似但不同；第 2 条是观察反应，本条是验证诚实 |
| 第 3 条 | access / boundary / changed distance | 否；本条不讲距离变化 |
| 第 4 条 | already knows / truth verification | 新变量 |

- 保持同一 AI 主播、深色背景、大字幕、约 55–60 秒长度和无口播 CTA。
- 不声称 Scorpio 能读心或一定知道答案。
- 不把”提问核对一致性”合理化为操控。
- 不使用心理诊断、创伤因果或绝对化 `Scorpio always`。

### 纯文本配音稿

```text
Scorpio will ask you a question they already know the answer to. They're not looking for information. They're checking whether you'll tell the truth.

And they won't ask it like an interrogation. It'll sound casual. Almost like they don't care about the answer. But they're watching every detail of how you respond.

The pause before you speak. Whether your story stays consistent. Whether your eyes shift when you get to the part they already know about. They're not gathering facts. They're measuring honesty.

Because for a Scorpio, the lie is always worse than the thing you're lying about. They can forgive the mistake. What they can't forgive is finding out you looked them in the face and chose to deceive them.

So when they ask you something small — something you think doesn't matter — pay attention. That's not small talk. That's a checkpoint. And your answer just told them everything they needed to know.

They didn't need your answer. They needed to see what you'd do with the truth.
```

预估时长：约 55–58 秒。

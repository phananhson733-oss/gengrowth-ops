# Libra People-Pleasing Reset

## Current synthesis
A Libra community discussion describes a sudden drop in the urge to keep caring for everyone, with commenters connecting the change to exhaustion, divorce and deliberately unlearning people-pleasing.

Main views: When people-pleasing has run for too long, saying no can initially feel cold or frightening even when it is a needed boundary correction.

## Life situations
- Someone who usually smooths everything over stops volunteering to fix the mood.
- After a divorce, a person practices choosing their own needs without apologizing.
- The new calm feels scary because it does not match the old people-pleasing identity.

## Language patterns
- "Same here. Got divorced a year ago and have been changing my people pleasing ways." — source_quote (src-0547f0cc5c)
- "A boundary reset can feel cold when self-abandonment used to feel normal." — synthesized_pattern

## Counterviews and limitations
Sudden indifference can also signal burnout or unresolved hurt; healthy boundaries still require clear communication rather than disappearing or devaluing others.

Limitations: One Reddit discussion; the emotional shift may reflect burnout, grief or personal development rather than Libra identity.

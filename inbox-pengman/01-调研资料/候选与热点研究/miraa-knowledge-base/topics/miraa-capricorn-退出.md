# Capricorn 退出前已下定决心

## Current synthesis
Capricorns describe leaving as a decision finished before the exit; they refuse reconciliation even when exes return.

Main views: The walk-away is final; everything was tried silently first; disrespected self-worth is the trigger.

## Life situations
- making up your mind before walking away
- hearing from mutual friends an ex wants you back
- giving every chance to fix it before leaving

## Language patterns
- "By the time I leave them I've made up my mind I won't be coming back." — source_quote (src-bd76081c7a)
- "I never go back to problems" — source_quote (src-bd76081c7a)

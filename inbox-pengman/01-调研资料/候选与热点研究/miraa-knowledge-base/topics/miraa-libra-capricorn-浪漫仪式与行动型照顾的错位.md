# Libra × Capricorn：浪漫仪式与行动型照顾的错位

## Current synthesis
In a Libra romance thread, one commenter contrasts a desire for romantic dinners, gifts and quiet sunsets with a Capricorn partner who expresses devotion through cooking and organizing birthday events.

Main views: Acts of service can carry real devotion, but they may not fully replace explicit romance or the feeling of being intentionally celebrated.

## Life situations
- A partner cooks, handles the house or organizes a birthday event instead of giving flowers.
- The other person appreciates the effort but still wants a romantic dinner or personal gift.
- Small remembered details may feel more meaningful than expensive public gestures.

## Language patterns

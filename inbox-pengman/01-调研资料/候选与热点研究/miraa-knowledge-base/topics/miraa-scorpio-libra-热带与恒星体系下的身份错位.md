# Scorpio × Libra：热带与恒星体系下的身份错位

## Current synthesis
A poll asks whether tropical Scorpios resonate with Libra under sidereal dates. The comments show mixed responses and repeatedly introduce other placements, including Scorpio Sun and Moon with Libra rising.

Main views: People may recognize traits from both labels because tropical and sidereal systems calculate signs differently and because a full chart contains multiple placements.

## Life situations
- A person learns that their Sun sign changes when they switch chart systems.
- They compare the new label with the sign identity they have used for years.
- Comments reveal that Moon, rising, Venus and stellium placements complicate a simple two-label comparison.

## Counterviews and limitations
Many voters rejected the Libra identification, while others attributed resonance to Libra placements. The poll cannot prove one zodiac system more accurate than the other.

Limitations: One subreddit poll; self-selection and system familiarity are unknown, and the result is not a scientific comparison of zodiac systems.

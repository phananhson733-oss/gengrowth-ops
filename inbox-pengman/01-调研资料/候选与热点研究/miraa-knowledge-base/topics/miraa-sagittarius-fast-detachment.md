# Sagittarius Fast Detachment

## Current synthesis
A Sagittarius community discussion centers on becoming intensely close, feeling a sudden ick after a surprising behavior, and then wanting to detach quickly. Some commenters view that switch as protection rather than a flaw.

Main views: Fast detachment can protect a real boundary, but the speed of the switch is worth checking before treating one disappointing moment as the whole relationship.

## Life situations
- Two people feel inseparable until one unexpected action changes the emotional temperature.
- The first reaction is to stop replying and mentally end the relationship.
- Later, the person tries to decide whether the ick exposed a boundary or only a moment of disappointment.

## Language patterns
- "On the contrary I have always seen my ability to turn on and turn off relationships as a bonus feature instead of something negative." — source_quote (src-208c92fe33)
- "A fast exit can be a boundary, but it can also skip the clarity check." — synthesized_pattern

## Counterviews and limitations
Some commenters experience quick emotional shutoff as a useful ability; leaving can be healthy when the surprise reveals disrespect or a repeated pattern.

Limitations: One sign-specific Reddit discussion; quick detachment can reflect context, attachment history or safety concerns rather than astrology.

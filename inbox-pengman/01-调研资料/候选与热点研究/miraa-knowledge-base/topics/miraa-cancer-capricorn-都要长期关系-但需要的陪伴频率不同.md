# Cancer × Capricorn：都要长期关系，但需要的陪伴频率不同

## Current synthesis
A Cancer community thread asks whether Cancer–Capricorn relationships can last. A detailed reply argues that both may want deep, enduring connection while differing sharply in how often presence, affection and reassurance need to be expressed.

Main views: One person may register love through frequent contact and shared daily activities, while the other may register commitment through focus, stability and occasional but durable action.

## Life situations
- One person wants daily conversation, hugs, walks or meals together to feel connected.
- The other stays focused on work and assumes the relationship remains secure without constant contact.
- Both want endurance, but one experiences silence as absence while the other experiences it as trust.

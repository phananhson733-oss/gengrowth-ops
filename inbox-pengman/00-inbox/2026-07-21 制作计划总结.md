## 类型一：纯字幕星座系列（Moon Sign Toxic Traits）

**账号：① AstrologyWiki 官方 @astrologywiki**
**格式：17秒 | 无口播 | 星座连线图背景 + 底部黑色半透明圆角字幕框 + 工具截图 CTA**
**工具：CapCut**
**参考视频：** @knoxstellar.official, @promentalityx

| #   | 选题                                            | 覆盖星座                       | 脚本/字幕核心               | 状态  |
| --- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------- | --- |
| 1   | Your toxic trait, according to your Moon sign | Scorpio / Gemini / Pisces  | 每个星座一句尖锐行为标签，底部字幕快速切换 | 已发布 |
| 2   | Your toxic trait — Fire Moon                  | Aries / Leo / Sagittarius  | 同上格式                  | 已发布 |
| 3   | Your toxic trait — Earth Moon                 | Taurus / Virgo / Capricorn | 同上格式                  | 已发布 |
| 4   | Your toxic trait — 收尾集                        | Cancer / Libra / Aquarius  | 同上格式；测试 A3 女性角色图视觉    | 已发布 |

### 视觉规格
- 深蓝/黑色星空背景，白色发光星点连线
- 顶部星座名称
- 底部黑色半透明圆角框内放行为描述文字
- 结尾 AstrologyWiki 工具截图作为 CTA
- 节奏：每个星座约 4-5 秒

### 字幕示例（第2集 Fire Moon）

```
Aries Moon — 你的 toxic trait：冲动决定完之后立刻后悔，但嘴上绝不认
Leo Moon — 你的 toxic trait：表面大方，其实暗暗记着谁没给你足够的认可
Sagittarius Moon — 你的 toxic trait：承诺太快，消失更快
```

| # | 选题 | Hook | 核心机制 | 状态 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Scorpio 第1条 — 先离开 | "The biggest problem with Scorpio is they'll leave before you get the chance to." | withdrawal / leave first | 已发布 |
| 8 | Scorpio 第2条 — 暗中测试 | （testing / watch your reaction） | 试探对方反应 | 已发布 |
| 9 | Scorpio 第3条 — 改变距离 | "A Scorpio won't always tell you something changed. They'll just quietly move you to the outside." | access / boundary | 已发布 |

### 视觉规格
- 深色录音棚氛围背景
- AI 主播 Mira：沉稳、温和、"懂你"的心理分析师声线
- 大字幕，关键词高亮
- 首帧必须完整可读（Hook 文字）

### 配音稿 — 第1条（~58秒）

```
The biggest problem with Scorpio is they'll leave before you get the chance to.

And most people think that's cruelty. That they just don't care enough to stay.

But for people with strong Scorpio placements, it tends to be the opposite. The more they care, the harder it becomes to stay open.

There's a pattern here. When closeness builds, something pulls back. Walk away first. Control the ending. Don't let anyone get close enough to leave you.

It looks like silence. It looks like walls. But underneath that — it's often a reflex around trust. Not cruelty. Protection.

In astrology, Pluto rules Scorpio. And Pluto is about extremes — total trust or total shutdown. Vulnerability or control. Transformation or destruction. There's rarely an in-between.

So when someone with heavy Scorpio energy goes quiet, it may not be indifference. It may be that staying feels more dangerous than leaving.

This isn't everyone with a Scorpio Sun. It depends on your Pluto, your Moon, your whole chart. But if you recognize this pattern — now you have a different lens for it.
```

### 配音稿 — 第3条（~57秒）

```
A Scorpio won't always tell you something changed. They'll just quietly move you to the outside.

They don't blow up. They don't give you the fight you're expecting. They just… stop giving you the version of them you used to get.

The late-night conversations get shorter. The things they used to share without thinking, they now keep to themselves. You're still in their life. But you're no longer in the inner circle.

And the hardest part? They won't explain it. Because in their mind, you already know what you did. And the fact that you're not addressing it is confirmation enough.

So they don't remove you. They just reclassify you. You went from someone who had full access… to someone who gets the surface.

And they'll be perfectly polite the entire time.
```

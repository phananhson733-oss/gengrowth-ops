## 1. Scope and stop point

Act as an English social-media community researcher. Research recent, actively discussed Scorpio topics that can inform `@miraaastrology` TikTok candidates for US users aged 18–35.

Use this research for Mode B weekly candidate planning and Mode C refill/replan. Return a research pool for Pengman to screen; do not select a final topic or begin content production.

Seek concrete identity, behavior, emotional experience, relationship interaction, work, friendship, family, trust, needs, self-protection, or life-conflict discussions that a non-expert can understand and a 45–90 second AI-host video could explain.

Do not treat broad labels such as “mysterious,” “loyal,” “possessive,” or “intense” as sufficient topics without a repeated real-life conflict or behavior.

## 2. Source and access rules

Attempt multiple English community sources:

- Reddit;
- X;
- Quora;
- public TikTok or YouTube comments/discussions;
- other relevant public English forums, Q&A sites, or social platforms.

Open actual posts, discussion pages, or comment threads. Search snippets, result titles, summaries, and page metadata are discovery aids only and cannot support a formal discussion point.

For every formal discussion point:

- retain at least one readable direct source link;
- record publication/page date when visible and the observation date;
- distinguish readable body, readable comments, metadata only, login required, blocked, or unavailable;
- never infer body content from a title;
- never invent engagement counts, comments, quotes, or user language.

If a platform is inaccessible, record the failed access layer and continue with other readable communities. Do not claim cross-platform repetition unless readable direct evidence exists on at least two platforms.

## 3. Recency and discussion signals

Prioritize sources in this order:

1. previous 90 days;
2. previous 12 months;
3. older material only to demonstrate a recurring long-term question, never current popularity.

Judge discussion level using available evidence:

- the same question appears independently in multiple posts;
- visible comments, replies, likes, votes, saves, or views indicate activity;
- similar experiences appear across communities;
- comments contain recognition, disagreement, or personal stories;
- recent independent users raise the same conflict.

If exact engagement is unavailable, write `互动量不可核验` and name the substitute signal used. Assign evidence strength `强 / 中 / 弱`; do not call a topic popular solely because it is interesting.

## 4. Research procedure

1. Read current product, account, capacity, inventory, recent-post, deduplication, safety, and `decision / next_test` context.
2. Read the mandatory competitor index and record `checked_at`; attempt the Apps Script Library as required by the parent Skill.
3. Search broadly for recent Scorpio discussions across the listed communities.
4. Open and read candidate pages and, when accessible, their comments or replies.
5. Cluster repeated questions by human conflict rather than astrology keyword.
6. Separate repeated evidence, single anecdotes, counterexamples, and unsupported inference.
7. Produce at least 5 distinct discussion points; target 8–12 when sufficient readable evidence exists. Do not pad weak items to meet the target.
8. Extract at least 15 concrete, visualizable life behaviors/scenes across the research pool.
9. Extract a language bank while distinguishing short verbatim user phrases from researcher synthesis. Keep quotes short and source-linked.
10. Save the research artifact before candidate generation. When Social OS is used, ingest usable sources/evidence before running candidate research.

## 5. Required topic fields

For each discussion point include:

1. **话题名称**：concise Chinese research label, not a TikTok Hook.
2. **用户在讨论什么**：actual questions, experiences, or disputes.
3. **具体生活场景**：2–4 repeated behaviors or situations from sources/comments.
4. **为什么适合做视频**：identity, recognition, comment, share, or send-to-someone potential; do not make the final selection.
5. **讨论度证据**：source count, cross-platform status, visible engagement or substitute signal, evidence strength, and direct links.
6. **主要观点与反方观点**：majority framing, objections, demographic/relationship/chart differences, and anecdote limits.
7. **美国用户自然语言**：label short real quotes separately from multi-source pattern summaries.

## 6. Required output

### Research receipt

Record:

- search time;
- platforms attempted and accessed;
- number of opened post/discussion pages;
- number with readable body and comments;
- unavailable sources and failure layers;
- sample limitations.

### High-discussion topic overview

Use:

| 编号 | 讨论话题 | 近期性 | 来源数量 | 跨平台情况 | 证据强度 | 视频化潜力 |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

List at least 5 supported topics; target 8–12. `视频化潜力` explains suitability only and does not choose a winner.

### Detailed topic research

Expand every topic using all seven required fields.

### Discussion tiers

- 第一层：近期多个来源反复出现；
- 第二层：有明显讨论，但样本有限；
- 第三层：有趣但主要来自单帖或少数经历。

### Life-detail bank

List at least 15 concrete behaviors or interaction scenes. Do not turn them into completed Hooks or scripts.

### Language bank

Provide:

- 15–20 natural English phrases;
- 5–10 common questions;
- 5–10 plausible comment-reaction patterns.

Mark every item as either a short source-linked quote or a synthesized pattern. Do not present invented wording as a user quote.

### Complete source list

For each source record title, platform/community, direct URL, displayed date, observation date, visible engagement, readability status, and supported discussion-point IDs.

## 7. Prohibited output

Do not generate:

- final TikTok Hooks;
- complete scripts;
- Captions or Hashtags;
- a final topic decision or “the only topic worth doing”;
- compatibility scores;
- unsupported trend claims;
- fabricated quotes, engagement, or community consensus.

Research provides multiple evidence-backed directions. Pengman makes the final selection; only the later candidate-generation step may convert selected research directions into candidate Hooks.

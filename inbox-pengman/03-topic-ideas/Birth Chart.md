## Why now

刚做完 Messi 热点内容，下一条适合回到长期基础主题。Birth chart 是首轮 P0 主题，也能自然承接 AstrologyWiki 的 “self-knowledge / patterns for reflection” 定位。

这条内容建议从用户自己的困惑切入，而不是从占星概念切入。英语区短视频里，泛用户更常说 `your sign`、`I'm a Leo`、`What's your sign?`，而 `zodiac sign` 更像标题 / 搜索 / 解释语境里的词。因此封面和开头可以用更口语的 `sign`，正文再解释它通常指的是 Sun sign。

Don't feel like your sign?

Most people meet astrology through one sentence:

“I’m a Leo.”  
“I’m a Scorpio.”  
“I’m a Gemini.”

That usually means your Sun sign.

And your Sun sign is important.

Think of it as one layer of a larger map.

Your birth chart adds the Moon, Rising, planets, houses, and aspects.

A simple way to read it:

Planets show **what** part of you is speaking.  
Signs show **how** that energy expresses itself.  
Houses show **where** it shows up in life.  
Aspects show **how** different parts of you interact.

Use the chart as a mirror for better questions and fuller self-understanding.

Start with your Sun, Moon, and Rising — then go one layer deeper.

Explore your birth chart at AstrologyWiki:  
https://www.astrologywiki.com/en/wiki/how-to-read-birth-chart

### Final Voiceover Script

```text
You probably know your zodiac sign.

Maybe you say, "I'm a Leo," or "I'm a Pisces," and that feels like astrology.

That's the starting point. A birth chart goes further.

A birth chart, also called a natal chart, is a map of the sky at the exact moment and place you were born. To create one, you usually need three things: your birth date, your birth time, and your birth location.

Your Sun sign is part of that map. It describes a core part of your identity and life direction. But it is only one piece.

Your chart also includes the Moon, the Rising sign, and the other planets. That is why two people with the same Sun sign can still feel completely different.

A simple way to begin is to think of a birth chart in four layers.

Planets describe what part of you is being expressed: your emotions, your communication style, your desires, your sense of purpose.

Signs describe how that energy comes through. Is it direct, careful, curious, intense, steady, expressive?

Houses show where that energy plays out in life: relationships, work, home, creativity, learning, or personal growth.

Aspects show how different parts of the chart interact with each other. Some flow easily. Some create tension. Both can teach you something.

If that sounds like a lot, you do not need to read the whole chart at once.

Start with your Big Three: Sun, Moon, and Rising.

Or pick one placement, like Moon in Virgo or Mars in Gemini, and learn what that one piece means.

A birth chart is best approached slowly. One symbol at a time. One pattern at a time.

If you want a beginner-friendly path, AstrologyWiki has a full guide on how to read your birth chart, including planets, signs, houses, and aspects.

Start with the basics, then follow the chart from there.
```

- Four-layer framework: planets / signs / houses / aspects 分别解释表达内容、表达方式、生活领域、内在互动。
- Beginner path: 先看 Big Three，或从一个 placement 慢慢开始。
- CTA: 引导到 AstrologyWiki 的 How to Read Your Birth Chart 页面继续阅读。

### Production Notes

- This is not a full astrology course; it is an entry guide.
- Do not explain all planets, all houses, or all aspects.

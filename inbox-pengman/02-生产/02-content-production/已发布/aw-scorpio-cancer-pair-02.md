## Script

**Hook (0-3s):**
Scorpio and Cancer love each other the way they wish someone had loved them.

**Body (3-50s):**
And that sounds beautiful until you realize what it actually means.

Cancer gives everything. The care, the check-ins, the "I noticed you seemed off today." They love you the way they needed to be loved when no one was paying attention.

Scorpio gives depth. The loyalty that doesn't waver. The kind of presence that says "I'm not going anywhere, even when this gets ugly." They love you the way they needed to be held when everyone else left.

So you have two people pouring from the exact place they were never filled.

And here's where it breaks. When Cancer gives you all that care, what they're really saying is: give this back to me. When Scorpio gives you all that depth, what they're really saying is: don't waste this.

Now both of them feel like they're giving more than they're getting. Both of them feel unseen. Both of them are exhausted by a love that should feel easy but never does.

**Closing (50-55s):**
They're not loving each other. They're filling something in themselves that never got filled.

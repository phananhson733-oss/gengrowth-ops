## 统一内容 Brief

- **目标用户**：美国 18–35 岁 TikTok 用户；Aquarius 本人，以及在关系中把伴侣独处误解为感情变淡的人。
- **内容目标**：把 Aquarius 的独处需求和关系中的失去兴趣区分开，同时保留伴侣需要明确沟通与 reassurance 的合理边界。
- **用户问题**：Aquarius 需要很多独处时间，是否代表他们不再在乎这段关系？
- **内容承诺**：解释空间可能用于整理想法，但空间不能代替沟通；健康的独立需要一句清楚的 reassurance。
- **核心机制**：`需要独处整理想法 → 伴侣把安静误读为感情变化 → 压力增加退缩 → 用明确 reassurance 降低不确定性`。
- **账号匹配理由**：单一星座、具体关系张力、双方都有合理需求，符合 Miraa 的心理与关系内容定位。
- **去重说明**：不同于 W34 Aquarius“慢回梗图、关键时刻接电话”的友情可靠性角度；本条只讨论亲密关系中的独处、压力与明确沟通。
- **事实边界**：这是面向 Aquarius 原型的关系解释，不代表所有 Aquarius 都有相同行为；不把失联、冷处理或无限期不确定合理化。

## 已确认 English Voiceover

```text
Aquarius can care about you and still need a lot of time alone.

That can be confusing when the relationship is going well. One day they're fully present, and the next they disappear into a project, a hobby, or their own thoughts. Their partner may assume the distance means their feelings changed.

But Aquarius often uses space to sort out what they think before they explain what they feel. Pressure can make them pull back even more, especially when every quiet moment becomes a relationship test.

That doesn't mean their partner should accept endless uncertainty. Aquarius still needs to say, “I need some time, and we're okay.” Independence works in a relationship when it includes reassurance, not when the other person has to guess.
```

今日 B2 选题：

**Why We Can't Stop Watching Rhaenyra Fight for Legitimacy**

推荐做成 35-45 秒 TikTok / YouTube Shorts / IG Reels 可复用短视频。形式上不需要高成本全数字人，优先用 **AI host 口播 + House of the Dragon 剧集截图/官方物料 + AstrologyWiki 页面录屏 + CapCut 字幕/图卡**。

内容目的：

- 借 House of the Dragon S3E3 的当周讨论热度，拉用户进入 AstrologyWiki 的影视角色 astrology 页面。

## 1. 最终脚本

英文口播版：

```text
House of the Dragon is tense right now because every character is fighting over one question: who gets to be seen as legitimate?

Rhaenyra may hold power, but power still has to be recognized by the city, the nobles, the family, and her own allies.

The tension comes from that gap between having a claim and getting people to accept it.

In astrology language, this is a 10th-house kind of pressure: public role, reputation, authority, and the burden of being watched while you lead.

There is also an 8th-house theme here: inheritance, family power, hidden debts, and the fear that someone else can take what was promised to you.

Rhaenyra becomes compelling through that pressure: power, recognition, and whether the world will accept her claim.

Read the Rhaenyra character astrology breakdown on AstrologyWiki.
```

30 秒压缩版：

```text
House of the Dragon is tense right now because Rhaenyra is fighting for recognition.

She may hold power, but power still has to be accepted by the city, the nobles, the family, and her own allies.

In astrology language, that is 10th-house pressure: reputation, authority, public role, and being watched while you lead.

There is also an 8th-house theme: inheritance, family power, hidden debts, and fear of replacement.

Her story carries the weight of a claim that keeps being challenged.

The tension lands here: who gets recognized as legitimate power?

Read the Rhaenyra character astrology breakdown on AstrologyWiki.
```

口播要求：

- 语气：dramatic but controlled / smart TV commentary / astrology-aware。
- 不讲剧情预测，不说角色命运会怎样。
- 不把 fictional character 当真实 natal chart 断言。
- 避免 `is not... it is...`、`not X but Y`、`not only...` 这类模板化反转句。
- 如果使用剧集画面，字幕和口播要明显是评论/解析，不做纯搬运剪辑。

## 3. 可用素材形式

### A. 剧集截图 / 官方图片

用途：

- 开头建立 HotD 语境。
- 0:03-0:14 展示王座、议会、权力压力、角色表情。
- 可做轻微 Ken Burns 推近，不需要复杂动效。

素材建议：

- 优先使用 HBO / Max 官方预告、官方剧照、官方社媒公开图。
- 可用自己合法观看时截取的短截图做评论性解析。
- 画面上必须加 AstrologyWiki 的评论字幕和图卡，不做无解说搬运。

注意：

- 避免连续长片段播放。
- 单个剧集片段尽量保持很短，优先静帧 + 口播。
- 不使用第三方粉丝剪辑水印素材。

### B. 剧集视频短片段

用途：

- 只做 1-2 个 1-2 秒过渡，例如议会场景、角色转头、王座氛围。
- 用在 0:00-0:08 或 0:23-0:32，增强时效感。

注意：

- 不要让剧集原声成为主体。
- 不要连续播放剧情关键片段。
- 口播、字幕、图卡要主导信息。

### C. AI Host

用途：

- 开头或解释段中出现 2-3 次，降低纯剧集素材依赖。
- 适合承接 10th house / 8th house 解释。

Host 设定：

```text
Mira, a modern AI astrology explainer host, speaking directly to camera with a calm dramatic TV-commentary tone, upper body portrait, simple dark navy outfit, subtle silver astrology necklace, soft studio lighting, minimal modern studio background with dark blue and muted gold accents, tasteful fantasy-drama atmosphere without copying any TV show, realistic natural skin texture, friendly but authoritative expression, vertical 9:16, centered composition, space above head for captions, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

Negative prompt：

```text
no text, no fake letters, no watermark, no celebrity likeness, no actor likeness, no dragon, no TV show logo, no HBO logo, no crown copied from House of the Dragon, no exaggerated fantasy costume, no witch hat, no crystal ball, no distorted face, no extra fingers, no plastic skin, no airbrushed skin
```

### D. AstrologyWiki 页面录屏 / 截图

用途：

- 结尾 CTA。
- 展示 `rhaenyra-targaryen-zodiac-sign` 页面标题、页面布局、或相关 character astrology 内容。

录屏方式：

- 浏览器打开页面。
- 竖屏裁切或移动端模拟。
- 录 4-6 秒慢滚动。
- CapCut 里加大字：`Rhaenyra character astrology` / `Read on AstrologyWiki`。

### E. CapCut 图卡

必须用 CapCut 做清晰文字，避免 AI 生成错字。

核心图卡：

```text
10th house = reputation + authority
8th house = inheritance + shared power
Power must be recognized
Who gets seen as legitimate?
```

视觉风格：

- 深蓝 / 黑 / 暗金 / 白字。
- 轻微火光或星点纹理可以有，但不要做成廉价奇幻风。
- 字体清楚，手机上 1 秒内能看懂。

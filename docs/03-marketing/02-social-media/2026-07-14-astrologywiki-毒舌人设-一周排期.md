## 一、核心理念：为什么走"毒舌"

### 参照对象：Co-Star 的「推送 meme 病毒」

Co-Star 每天给用户推一条**又毒、又准、有点冒犯**的运势短句（例："It's not that deep. Take a nap."）。用户被"被一个 App 拷打/说中"戳到 → 截图发 IG/X → 变成 meme → 别人好奇也去下载。**这套内容人格是 Co-Star 最大的免费获客来源，不花投放钱自己滚雪球。**

### astrologywiki 的差异化：毒舌 + 心理学 + 反玄学

我们**不是**照抄 Co-Star 的纯毒舌，而是叠加自己的定位护城河：

| 竞品做法 | astrologywiki 的人设 |
|---|---|
| Co-Star：越玄、越随机、越 mean 越好 | 懂点**心理学**、又**不信玄学**的损友 |
| 普通占星：温吞正能量（"今天适合社交😊"）| 用星象当由头，戳的其实是你的**心理防御** |
| 玄学号：预测吉凶、宿命论 | **祛魅**——"不是水逆，是你手贱" |

> **一句话人设定义**：一个会拆穿你的损友——它不哄你（区别于正能量占星），也不装神弄鬼（区别于玄学号）。星象只是由头，它真正戳的是你在逃避的那件事。

### 机制要点（运营必记）

1. **人格 > 信息**：中性百科在社媒会哑火。只有语气像"会拆穿你的损友"，内容才有转发欲。
2. **反玄学是我们的专属味道**：Co-Star 写不出"不是星星辜负你，是那个人"这种句子——越祛魅越是差异化，这类句子优先多产。
3. **戳行为、不戳身份**：可以说"你在逃避"，不要说"你没救了"。冒犯要停在"自嘲线"上。

## 三、一周排期

> 时间按**美东（ET）**，目标美国用户活跃时段。主句英文，脚本分镜中文。

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 周一 | TikTok | Saturn return = the bill for the last ten years | 土星回归计算器 | 工具导流 |
| 周二 | IG Reel | Mercury isn't retrograde… | 行运追踪页 | 反玄学·纯传播 |
| 周三 | X 推文 | Reading your ex's birth chart… surveillance | 合盘 synastry | 工具·自嘲 |

### 周一 · TikTok · 土星回归

**钩子句**：*"Your Saturn return isn't a crisis. It's the bill for the last ten years."*

- **0–3s 钩子**：真人正脸/文字卡念主句，语气冷静、有点欠。
- **4–12s 展开**（字幕，英文旁白）：*"Saturn takes about 29.5 years to return to where it was when you were born. Around 27 to 30, it comes back. That 'everything is falling apart' feeling? Not a curse."*
- **13–24s 心理学落点**：*"It's your psyche auditing every rushed choice you made in your twenties. Career, relationships, who you pretended to be."*
- **25–30s CTA**：*"Find your exact Saturn return date — link in bio. It's free."*
- **配文**：Your twenties are ending on schedule. 🪐 #saturnreturn #astrology #psychology
- **短链**：→ 土星回归计算器

### 周二 · IG Reel · 反玄学（纯传播）

**钩子句**：*"Mercury isn't retrograde. You just sent a text you shouldn't have."*

- **0–3s 钩子**：主句配"发送后瞬间后悔"的表情/画面。
- **4–15s 展开**：*"Mercury retrograde is real astronomy — the planet looks like it's moving backwards. What it is NOT: an excuse for your 2 a.m. text to your ex."*
- **16–26s 落点**：*"Blaming the sky feels better than admitting you were lonely. That's not astrology. That's avoidance."*
- **27–30s 软 CTA**：*"We do astrology without the excuses. → link in bio."*
- **配文**：It was you. It's always been you. 💀 #mercuryretrograde #astrologymemes
- **短链**：→ 行运追踪页

### 周三 · X 推文 · 合盘

**推文正文**：
> *"Reading your ex's birth chart again?*
> *That's not astrology. That's surveillance.*
> *(we built the tool anyway 👀)"*

- 玩法：X 单条纯文字最好传播。括号自嘲"工具我们照做了"是钩子——承认大家都偷偷查前任，反而更想点。
- 配图（可选）：合盘工具截图打码，营造"偷看"感。
- **短链**：→ 合盘 synastry
- 备注：可原样搬 Reddit r/astrology（去短链，纯玩梗涨脸熟，简介引流）。

### 周四 · TikTok · 行运（心理学最正）

**钩子句**：*"Transits don't happen to you. You just finally noticed the pattern."*

- **0–3s 钩子**：念主句，配"恍然大悟"定格。
- **4–14s 展开**：*"A transit is just where the planets are today, compared to your birth chart. It doesn't 'do' anything to you."*
- **15–25s 落点**：*"What it does is give you a date to finally admit the thing you've been ignoring for months. The planet is a permission slip, not a cause."*
- **26–30s CTA**：*"See what's transiting your chart right now — free, no sign-up. Link in bio."*
- **配文**：The pattern was always yours. #astrology #selfawareness #transits
- **短链**：→ 行运追踪页

### 周五 · IG Reel + X · 引战

**钩子句**：*"'I'm just an air sign' is not a personality. It's an excuse."*

- **0–3s 钩子**：直视镜头念出，故意欠打。
- **4–16s 展开**：*"Your sun sign is one placement out of dozens. Leading with 'I'm a Gemini' to explain why you flake? That's not your chart. That's a choice."*
- **17–27s 落点+反转**：*"Real astrology doesn't let you off the hook. It shows you the pattern so you can actually change it."*
- **28–30s CTA**：*"Your full chart is more than three words. → link in bio."*
- **配文**：say it louder for the flaky Geminis in the back 🌬️ #astrology #geminis #bigthree
- **短链**：→ 免费星盘
- 备注：**故意引星座党吵架**，评论区越吵曝光越高，别删负评，回怼即可（保持损友人设）。

### 周六 · TikTok + Pinterest · 反玄学（周末高峰）

**钩子句**：*"The stars didn't ghost you. A person did. Own it."*

- **0–4s 钩子**：慢镜头+主句字幕，情绪偏丧偏真诚（本条不搞笑，走共鸣）。
- **5–18s 展开**：*"Astrology can tell you why you keep picking the same emotionally unavailable person. It can't text them back for you."*
- **19–27s 落点**：*"Use the chart to understand yourself. Not to wait for the universe to fix a person who already left."*
- **28–30s 软收**：*"Grounded astrology. Link in bio when you're ready."*
- **Pinterest 版**：同句做**深色极简图卡**（大字主句+底部小字 astrologywiki.com），标题 "Why you keep choosing unavailable people (astrology + psychology)"，pin 描述埋 birth chart 关键词——**这条兼吃 GEO/长尾**。
- **短链**：TikTok 无 / Pinterest 挂 → 免费星盘

### 周日 · YouTube Short + 全平台复用 · 产品钩子收口

**钩子句**：*"Knowing your Big Three won't fix your attachment style. But it's a start."*

- **0–4s 钩子**：念主句，语气比平时软一点（周日情绪，留口子）。
- **5–18s 展开**：*"Your Big Three — sun, moon, rising — get all the attention. But they're the trailer, not the movie."*
- **19–27s 落点+升维**：*"Your moon shows how you need to be loved. Your Venus shows how you actually love. That's where the real work — and the real astrology — starts."*
- **28–30s 强 CTA**：*"Read your full chart free — no sign-up to start. Link below."*
- **配文**：Start with the whole chart, not the trailer. #bigthree #astrology #attachmentstyle
- **短链**：→ 免费星盘（本周转化收口，导注册）

## 四、执行备注

| 项 | 建议 |
|---|---|
| **发布时间（ET）** | 工作日 12:00–13:00 或 19:00–21:00；周末 10:00–12:00。别按国内时间发。 |
| **短链** | 统一用一个短链域（如 `awiki.co/saturn`、`/synastry`、`/chart`），每条带 UTM，**才能在 GA4 里看清哪条句子真的带来了注册**——直接补当前最大的数据盲区。 |
| **一条素材多平台复用** | TikTok 竖版直接搬 IG Reel / YouTube Short；X 用纯文字版；Pinterest 用图卡版。1 个脚本产 4 个平台。 |
| **人设护栏** | 戳行为不戳身份；反玄学是专属味道（周一/二/六优先）；引战贴（周五）不删负评、回怼保持损友感。 |
| **别每条硬广** | 一周 7 条里 4 条挂工具、3 条纯情绪/引战，比例别失衡，否则掉粉。 |

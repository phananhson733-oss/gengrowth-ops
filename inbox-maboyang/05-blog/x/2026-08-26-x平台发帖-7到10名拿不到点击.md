# X 平台发帖 · 别用一个平均CTR看你的排名

## v2 改了什么

v1 的第一行是"Checked my own rankings"，整条推文的主语是"我"，读者读完知道的是"这个人的站怎么样"，拿不走任何自己能用的东西。

v2 把主语换成读者能执行的动作：**你的整体CTR是一个被平均出来的假象，拆成两个档位看**。我方数字挪到举例的位置，用来证明这个方法真能挖出东西，不再是全文的重点。

## 一、主推：单条测水

> Your overall CTR is hiding two very different stories.
>
> Split your rankings into two bands: positions 1-6, and 7-10. Compare each band's CTR against published position-level benchmarks, not the blended average.
>
> When I did this, band one was slightly ahead of benchmark. Band two was 73% below it. The blended number looked fine.

约290字符，单条装得下。

## 二、完整 Thread（4条）

### 1/

> If you're checking rankings against CTR benchmarks, do it by position band, not as one blended average. The average can hide a real problem in one band while the other band looks fine.

### 2/

> Pull your GSC data, split into positions 1-6 and 7-10, and get the average CTR for each band separately. Then compare each one to the published benchmark for that position range, not to a single overall number.

### 3/

> Example from doing this myself: band one (avg rank 5.1) came in at 5.54% CTR against a 5.1% benchmark, basically on target. Band two (avg rank 8.7) came in at 0.53% against a benchmark closer to 2.0%. 73% below.

### 4/

> The blended average across both bands looked unremarkable. Splitting it is what showed where the real gap was. If you've never split yours, that's the five-minute check worth doing before anything else.

## 三、取舍说明

| 处理 | 内容 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| **主语换人** | 单条版第一句从"我查了我的排名"改成"你的整体CTR藏着两个故事" | 用户反馈：要给读者能用的东西，不是讲我们自己的发现 |
| **我方数字降级** | 从标题数据变成thread第3条的"举例"，且明确写"example from doing this myself" | 数字仍然真实可信，但不再是全文重点，重点是读者能照做的方法 |
| **结尾换成行动号召** | "如果你没拆过，这是排在所有事情前面、五分钟能做完的检查" | 给读者一个具体的、今天能做的动作，不是甩出结论就结束 |
| **保留** | 两个档位各自的均排/CTR/基准三个数字 | 具体数字才让"拆开看"这个方法显得可信，不是空泛建议 |

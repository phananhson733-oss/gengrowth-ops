## 二、完整 Thread（5 条）

### 1/

> Ahrefs ran 863K SERPs against 4M AI Overview URLs in March.
>
> 37.9% of cited pages rank top 10.
> 31.2% sit at 11-100.
> 31% don't rank in the top 100 at all.
>
> Their July 2025 study had top 10 at 76%.

### 2/

> Seven months, 76% to 38%. Ranking and getting cited came apart faster than most of the advice has caught up with.

### 3/

> Their explanation is query fan-out.
>
> Google splits your query into sub-queries, runs each one, and cites whatever keeps reappearing across those results.
>
> What matters is how many sub-questions you're the best answer to. Not where you sit on the head term.

### 4/

> Which flips the work.
>
> A page at rank 30 that nails four of the sub-questions beats a #1 that only covers the head term.

### 5/

> Practical version: open the People Also Ask box on your target query. Treat it as the fan-out list.
>
> Every question in there needs something on your site that answers it directly and can be lifted out on its own.

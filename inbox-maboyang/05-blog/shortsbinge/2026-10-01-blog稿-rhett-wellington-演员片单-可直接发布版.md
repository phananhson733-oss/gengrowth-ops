## Start Here: Luca Vitiello in Bound by Honor

[Bound by Honor](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/bound-by-honor-dwvgmfmiu4dc/) is the role most people know him for, and with 477 IMDb votes it's the most-watched title on this list by a wide margin. It adapts *Bound by Honor*, the first novel in Cora Reilly's Born in Blood Mafia Chronicles. Aria, a sheltered daughter of the Chicago mafia, is married off to Luca, the cold heir of the New York mafia, and has to decide whether giving him her heart would be a mistake.

Because it's based on a well-loved book, the role came with expectations attached: readers already had a picture of Luca in their heads. The 7.9 rating across nearly 500 votes suggests the adaptation, and his take on Luca, landed with that audience. It's also long, at 93 episodes, with 11 free, so it's the one to pick if you want a full-scale saga rather than a quick binge.

## Luca Didn't Stop at One Series

Bound by Honor clearly worked, because Luca kept coming back. IMDb credits Rhett Wellington as Luca Vitiello again in two follow-ups, *Bound by Love* (7.9 from 156 votes) and *Bound by Duty* (7.4 from 75 votes), neither of which is on ShortsBinge yet.

There's also a smaller crossover. IMDb lists him as Luca in a single episode of [Sweet Temptation](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/sweet-temptation-jzdpoh6kpvy6/), a separate mafia romance about Giulia, who is promised to a feared crime lord and must take the place of his late wife. It isn't his show. Talia Wynzel and Jesse Morales lead it. But if you've finished Bound by Honor and miss Luca, it's a fun place to spot him.

## His Highest-Rated Role: Monster in His Eyes

If you only want his best-reviewed work, go straight to [Monster in His Eyes](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/monster-in-his-eyes-6pm3u6w3vh52/). At 8.0 from 180 votes, it's his highest-rated drama. He plays Naz Conti Vitale, a mafia boss and ex-con who rescues Karissa Reed. Karissa is hemmed in by a controlling mother and an abusive ex, and after the rescue she dares him to let her into his world.

It's another mafia role, but a rougher one than Luca. Naz isn't a polished heir raised for the part. He's a man with a record, and the story leans on the danger of that. It also offers 13 free episodes, one of the longer free runs on this list.

## The Role That Asks the Most of Him: My Duplicated Husband

[My Duplicated Husband](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/my-duplicated-husband-i6x65tanlql2/) is the one to watch if you want to see his range. IMDb credits him with two characters, Carl and Killian, which fits the premise. The morning after Esther buries the husband who made her life a nightmare, he walks back in alive, gentle, and nothing like the man she put in the ground.

Playing both a cruel husband and his unsettling, kinder double is a very different job from the brooding mafia leads, and it's one of his best-rated titles at 7.9 from 168 votes. It also has the most free episodes of any of his dramas here, 15, so it costs nothing to see whether the twist pulls you in.

## His Werewolf Roles: One King, One Husband You'll Hate

He has two werewolf dramas in the library, and they're close to opposites.

In [Reborn for the Lycan King](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/reborn-for-the-lycan-king-mdve2xmuwvyp/) he's the hero. Diana is reborn after her stepsister's deadly betrayal, chooses her mate by shooting an arrow, and it lands on Alexander, the hidden Lycan King. It's a classic second-chance fantasy with him as the powerful, concealed ruler, rated 7.4 from 148 votes.

In [Leaving My Alpha Husband in Ice Cold Regret](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/leaving-my-alpha-husband-in-ice-cold-regret-g3rurues6btg/) he's the reason the heroine leaves. Snow catches her Alpha husband, Ashton, cheating, offers herself as the pack's frozen sacrifice, snaps their mate bond and leaves him to live with the regret. It's his lowest-rated title at 6.3. That's worth reading in context: he's playing the man viewers are meant to resent, and regret stories depend on how much the audience enjoys watching him lose.

## His Billionaire Romances

Three more titles put him in the billionaire mold, each with a different twist.

[Corrupting My Billionaire Boss's Heart](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/corrupting-my-billionaire-boss-s-heart-mkadafftnwav/) is the lightest of his dramas, tagged "easy watch" on ShortsBinge. He plays Royce, the straitlaced billionaire whose life is turned upside down when Lyla Walker starts work at his exclusive country club, unaware of a promise he once made to someone close to her. He shares the screen with Hannah Lowery here.

[I'll Make Your Daddy Mine](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/i-ll-make-your-daddy-mine-xnch7bhhat7l/) is a revenge romance. Lenore Williams sets out to get back at Ada Thornton, who stole her fiancé and killed her father, by targeting Ada's own father, an enigmatic mogul. IMDb lists Rhett as Arthur; going by the premise and his billing as the male lead, that's most likely the mogul father at the center of Lenore's plan. It's one of his newest titles, so its 7.2 rating comes from only 48 votes so far.

In [Honey-Trapped My Fiancé's Billionaire Rival](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/honey-trapped-my-fiance-s-billionaire-rival-rgm6m4la6yv4/) he's part of a larger cast rather than the lead. Jade Gray is asked by her partner of ten years to seduce his billionaire rival, Asher Blackwood, and it turns out Asher has wanted her for a long time. IMDb doesn't list his character, so we can't say which part he plays.

## Before ReelShort: A Long Screen Career

Short dramas aren't where his career started. Credited earlier as Rhett Wellington Ramirez, and in one very early role as Rhett Ramirez, he has a long list of film and TV credits on IMDb. They include guest roles in *2 Broke Girls* (2017) and *9-1-1: Lone Star* (2020), three episodes of *Grand Hotel* (2019), and the recurring part of Simon Talbot across the *Gabriel's Inferno* and *Gabriel's Redemption* films (2020–2023). More recently he appeared in *Jamojaya* (2023) and the horror anthology *V/H/S/Beyond* (2024).

He also works behind the camera. IMDb credits him as a writer, director, producer, editor and composer on a string of independent short films. That behind-the-scenes experience is unusual among vertical-drama leads, most of whom are newer to the screen.

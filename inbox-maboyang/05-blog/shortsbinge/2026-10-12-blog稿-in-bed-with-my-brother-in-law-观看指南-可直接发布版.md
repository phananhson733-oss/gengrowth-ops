# In Bed with My Brother-in-Law: The Gotti Pact, Two Brothers, and Bella's Three-Day Deadline

**Meta description:** Bella catches her fiancé cheating on their wedding day, spends one night with a stranger, and learns he's the mafia boss brother. The pact, the Gotti brothers, the free episodes and whether there's a Part 2.

---

Bella Miller is standing at the altar in her wedding dress, and her groom never shows up. When she goes looking for him, she finds him with another woman. By the end of the night she has called off the wedding, gotten drunk and spent the night with a handsome stranger she thinks is an escort.

He isn't. He's Damian Gotti, the most powerful mafia boss in the city and the older brother of the man she was supposed to marry. [In Bed with My Brother-in-Law](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/in-bed-with-my-brother-in-law-a635p3z5v33r/) is a forbidden mafia romance built on that one terrible coincidence. This guide explains the family pact that traps Bella, how the two Gotti brothers compare, where the free episodes leave you and the questions people keep searching, including whether there's a novel or a Part 2.

*A note before you start: the series includes sexual content, a scene in which the heroine is attacked by a group of men, and threats against a teenage girl. It's a dark romance, even though ReelShort labels it suitable for all ages.*

## The Pact That Started It All

The story rests on an old agreement. According to [ReelShort's character and story guide](https://www.reelshort.com/fandom/in-bed-with-my-brother-in-law-dailymotion-01-8-4d1e2a7a0873/ "nofollow"), the Miller and Gotti families are bound by a marriage pact that has lasted a hundred years. Under its terms, the Miller daughters belong to the Gotti sons.

Bella is a fallen heiress of the Miller family, and the pact makes her Hunter Gotti's fiancée whether she likes it or not. It's the reason she can't simply walk away when Hunter humiliates her, and the reason the story keeps pulling her back into the Gotti household.

## A Wedding Day Gone Wrong

The opening is fast and brutal. Bella waits at the altar. Hunter is a no-show. After the guests give up and leave, she goes looking for him and finds him with his cousin, Mia Vance.

Bella refuses to become a laughing stock. She confronts Hunter, calls off the engagement and walks out. Then, drunk and determined to get even, she decides to spend the night with an escort. That escort is Damian Gotti, Hunter's older brother, though Bella has no idea who he is.

It's the classic revenge one-night stand, with a mafia twist. She meant to hurt the man who betrayed her. Instead she has tied herself even more tightly to his family.

## How Damian Figures It Out First

One detail makes the setup work: Damian learns the truth long before Bella does. According to ReelShort's fandom coverage, he was escaping danger that night when the drunk bride mistook him for the escort she'd asked for. She had no idea who she'd found.

The next morning Bella is gone. What she leaves behind is Hunter's black card. That's how Damian discovers he has just spent the night with his brother's bride. From episode 2 onward, he knows exactly who she is, and she still thinks he's a stranger she paid for.

That imbalance drives the first third of the series. Every scene between them plays differently because one of them knows the secret and the other doesn't.

## Dragged Back into the Gotti Family

Bella wants nothing more to do with the Gottis, but Hunter has leverage. The fandom guide explains that he threatens to take her fifteen-year-old sister as his bride instead. Faced with that, Bella has to swallow her pride, beg Hunter to marry her after all and even make a public apology for calling off the engagement.

This is where ShortsBinge's Morals and Ethics tag comes in. Bella's choices are never simply about romance. She's protecting her sister, her family name and herself, inside a system that treats her as property.

## Meeting Damian Again

Hunter decides to introduce his returning bride to his powerful brother. Before the dinner, Bella runs into Damian and still assumes he's the escort from that night. She even threatens him with her family's name, mistaking him for a gold digger.

The truth arrives at the dinner table. The man she spent the night with is her future brother-in-law, and the most dangerous man in the family. Hunter has no idea. Damian, who has already worked out exactly who Bella is, does nothing to make the evening easier for her.

## Hunter and Damian, Side by Side

The whole story is built on the contrast between the two Gotti brothers.

| | Hunter Gotti | Damian Gotti |
|---|---|---|
| Position | The second son, Bella's fiancé under the pact | The elder brother and head of the mafia family |
| What he does to Bella | Cheats on her, humiliates her, threatens her sister | Pursues her and protects her |
| How he sees her | As something owed to him by the pact | As the woman he wants above all others |
| What he relies on | The pact and his elite friends | His power, though deadly enemies want him dead |

ReelShort's guide describes Damian as the most powerful man in America, a ruthless mafia king who owns half of New York and has deadly enemies on every side. Countless women want his attention, yet the only one he wants is the one he isn't supposed to have.

## Saving Damian, and a Three-Day Deadline

The turning point comes when Damian is attacked and Bella is the one who saves his life. The fandom guide points out the irony: if she had simply let him die, most of her problems would have disappeared. She risks her life for him anyway. Then she uses the moment to try to close the door. As far as she's concerned, their affair was a one-night deal.

Damian doesn't accept that. He gives her three days to call off her engagement to Hunter and become his woman, and he makes clear that it isn't a negotiation.

The deadline is what gives the first half of the series its tension. Bella has to deal with a fiancé who controls her family's fate and a brother-in-law who won't take no for an answer, all while hiding the one secret that could explode the whole family.

## Is There a Novel or a Part 2?

Both questions come up a lot. ReelShort presents the series as an original production, and its own FAQ describes it as an AI-generated animated original. A similarly titled story, "In Bed with My Ex's Brother-in-Law," also turns up in searches, but it's a different title.

As for Part 2, the series is a complete 45-episode story, and no sequel has been announced. ReelShort's official synopsis gives away the destination: Damian eventually exposes his brother's crimes, saves Bella, and the two unite to defend their mafia throne. Clips labeled "Part 2" on YouTube, TikTok or Facebook are usually later episodes of the same series.

# Taming My Bullies: Emma Parker, Rowan Calloway and the Big Four Who Picked the Wrong Girl

**Meta description:** Emma Parker transfers to Maple Elite Academy and punches back at the Big Four on day one. What happens in the 18 free episodes, who's really behind the bullying, and how Seasons 2 and 3 continue the story.

---

Emma Parker is sitting on a classroom floor, soaked to the skin, while half the school laughs at her. Rowan Calloway leans down and offers her a way out: apologize in front of everyone, and agree to be his personal servant.

She tells him to come a little closer. He does. A second later he's flat on his back, and she's the one doing the talking.

That's the moment [Taming My Bullies](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/taming-my-bullies-djfncnmzxyam/) shows you who its heroine is. This 81-episode ReelShort series takes the most familiar setup in vertical drama, the scholarship girl versus the richest boys in school, and hands it to a girl who answers every humiliation with her fists. It's loud, it's petty, and it's a lot of fun.

## The Deal That Got Her Into Maple Elite

Emma doesn't want to be at Maple Elite Academy. On her first morning she begs her mom to let her stay home from the "stupid, stuck-up" school. Her mom won't budge, because the spot came at a terrible price.

Emma's father, Robert Parker, died in a construction accident. The academy's people show up at the family's door with condolences and an offer of $800,000. Emma wants to throw them out. Her mom takes the money on one condition: Emma gets a place at the school, which has the best fencing team in the country. Her father always dreamed of seeing her win gold.

It's a quick setup, and you can watch it all in the first two minutes of [episode 1](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/taming-my-bullies-djfncnmzxyam/episode/1/). But it matters later. When Emma wants to quit, her mom reminds her that her father might have gone pro himself if he hadn't become a parent so young, and that Emma has his talent. And when Rowan's crowd breaks a brooch she treasures, she tells him it's all she has left of her father.

## Welcome to the Big Four's School

Within minutes of arriving, Emma is splashed by Rowan's car and her uniform is ruined. A girl named Hazel Jones rescues her with a spare T-shirt and a crash course in how Maple Elite works. The school motto, as Hazel tells it: money speaks louder than your GPA, and looks are your currency.

Then Hazel points out the Big Four, the heirs who really run the place:

1. **Liam Davenport**, heir to the famous Davenport family.
2. **Karl Reed**, whose grandfather was an arms dealer. Rumor says his family has mafia ties.
3. **August Langford**, the quiet one. His family owns over a dozen auction houses, and he's a gifted artist himself.
4. **Rowan Calloway**, the leader. His family's conglomerate stretches across all seven continents. As Hazel puts it, they're richer than the people on Forbes and probably own Forbes.

Emma's reaction is the right one: so they're just poor little rich boys? Hazel's warning is less funny. Make Rowan angry, and your life at this school is over. Even the teachers are scared of them.

Emma makes him angry before lunch.

## The Girl Who Hits Back

On her first day, Emma walks in on Rowan beating up a quieter student named Noah. She smacks Rowan, tells him that being rich doesn't give him the right to treat people like dirt, and calls him pathetic in front of a crowd. When Hazel begs her to back off, Emma shrugs. She's a champion fencer. They should be scared of her.

That attitude carries the free episodes. When Rowan's friends plan a prank involving a snake in her locker, Emma storms into his fencing practice, takes him down and tells him to handle his problems face to face. When he demands her apology on that classroom floor, she flips him instead. When someone breaks her father's brooch, she tracks him down, beats him in front of his friends and leaves with a threat so specific his friends can't stop laughing about it.

Plenty of viewers say this is exactly why they stuck with the show. Reviewers on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt38628990/ "nofollow"), where Season 1 holds a 7.4 rating from 467 votes, keep praising the same thing: Emma has a real backbone and stands up for herself. One even says the bully scenes are less cringeworthy than most vertical dramas. Not everyone is sold, though. One reviewer gave it 2 stars and said the chemistry between Emma and either boy didn't land for them.

## Rowan's Ego Is the Comedy

If Emma is the show's spine, Rowan's ego is its sense of humor. Every time she humiliates him, he finds a way to read it as flirting.

After she flips him in class, he sulks at home, outraged that anyone could think he's obsessed with a "normie peasant girl." After she tells him she's never dated anyone or even held hands, he decides that's practically a confession. She came all the way to his house to tell him she's single, he reasons. She must be into him. So he does the logical thing. He gathers the whole school and announces that Emma Parker may now be his girlfriend and his date to the homecoming dance.

Her answer is a hard no. Rich and hot, she tells him, but rotten to the core. Then she lists everything he's done to her.

If you love this kind of push and pull, the [Enemies to Lovers collection](https://shortsbinge.com/browse/story-beats/enemies-to-lovers/) is full of couples who start out exactly like this. What makes Rowan fun to watch is that the show keeps dropping hints he isn't who Emma thinks. One afternoon she catches him with a kitten, apparently tormenting it, and gets ready to tear into him. Then she realizes he's gently freeing it.

## Who's Really Behind It?

Emma's list of Rowan's crimes is long. By episode 18, the free episodes have quietly started taking it apart.

| What Emma blames Rowan for | What the free episodes show |
|---|---|
| Ruining her uniform on her first morning | That one really was him |
| Beating up Noah for no reason | Rowan had a reason, and it involves the photos below |
| Sending thugs to attack her and break her things | Rowan wanted a prank, not an assault, and kicks the guy who went too far out of school |
| Spreading rumors that she sleeps around | Rowan has no idea what she's talking about |
| Taking inappropriate photos of her at her locker | Noah admits he was the one behind them |

August is the first to hint at this. After Emma's fight at fencing practice, he hands her a drink, and she tells him he's not like the others, since he didn't join in when they were hurting Noah. His answer: not everything is what it seems, especially Noah.

The free episodes end right as that warning comes due. Emma, crying at homecoming, accepts a drink from Noah. He finally admits who took the locker photos, and that it was the reason Rowan went after him. Back at the party, Rowan's friends notice Noah wandering around and realize something is wrong. Rowan goes looking for her. Where that leads is waiting in episode 19.

## Homecoming in a Caterpillar Costume

The most painful scene in the free run is also the funniest. After Emma rejects Rowan in front of the school, Chelsea and her clique, the Big Four's self-appointed cheer squad, show up acting like her new best friends. They tip her off about the homecoming dress code. It's very exclusive, they say.

Emma arrives in a green caterpillar onesie with her face painted green. Everyone else is in sequins.

Rowan's response is the closest thing to a turning point in the free episodes. He has something dumped all over Chelsea's crew and announces that nobody touches Emma Parker. She's under his protection. When he finds her crying in the bathroom and offers to get her a new dress, she tells him she wouldn't take his help if everyone else on earth dropped dead. You'll be on her side and his at the same time, which is exactly where the show wants you.

## August or Rowan?

Rowan gets the big gestures, but August Langford keeps turning up in the quiet moments. He's the one who stops the guys attacking Emma, the one who checks if she's okay, and the one who seems to know more than he's saying. The series keeps hinting that he and Emma may have met before.

That split shows up in the reviews too. One IMDb reviewer argued that Emma actually has more chemistry with August than with Rowan, and said the love triangle is what kept the plot interesting. It runs 76 episodes, with 16 free.

## FAQ

**What is Taming My Bullies about?**
Transfer student Emma Parker clashes with the Big Four, the rich heirs who run Maple Elite Academy, on her first day. Their leader, Rowan Calloway, makes her his main target, while quiet August Langford keeps helping her.

**How many episodes does Taming My Bullies have?**

Meg Bush plays Emma Parker, and Cameron Porras plays Rowan Calloway. Luke Dodge plays August Langford, and Bar Daniel plays Hazel Jones.

**Is there a Taming My Bullies season 2?**
Yes. Taming My Bullies 2 (76 episodes) and Taming My Bullies 3 (65 episodes) continue the story, and Season 3 is the finale.

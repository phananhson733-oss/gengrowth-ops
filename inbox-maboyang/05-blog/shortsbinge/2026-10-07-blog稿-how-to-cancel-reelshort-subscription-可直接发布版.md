# How to Cancel a ReelShort Subscription (and Stop the Charges for Good)

**Meta description:** Step-by-step instructions to cancel ReelShort VIP on iPhone, Android and the app itself, plus the billing-date rule most people miss, what happens to your coins, and how refunds work.

---

ReelShort's VIP plans renew automatically, and the weekly plan renews every seven days. As a result, "how to cancel ReelShort" is one of the most common questions people ask about the app, and why so many complaints about it involve charges that kept coming after someone thought they'd stopped paying.

The good news is that cancelling takes about a minute once you know where to look. The catch is that where to look depends on how you paid, and deleting the app doesn't cancel anything. This guide covers every route, the timing rule in ReelShort's own terms that decides whether you get charged one more time, and what to do if you want your money back.

*All policy details below come from [ReelShort's Terms of Use](https://www.reelshort.com/web-terms.html "nofollow") (last updated October 20, 2025) and Apple's and Google's official help pages, checked on September 30, 2026.*

## First: Deleting the App Does Not Cancel Your Subscription

This is the single most common mistake, so it's worth saying on its own. A ReelShort VIP subscription is a recurring payment attached to your Apple account, your Google account or your card. Removing the app from your phone does nothing to that payment. The charges will continue until the subscription itself is cancelled.

Deleting your ReelShort account doesn't reliably stop it either. ReelShort's terms say that if you delete your account while subscribed, you can keep using the subscription for the period you've paid for. Cancel first, then delete the app or account if you want to.

## Step One: Work Out Where You Subscribed

Before you cancel, figure out which company is actually billing you. Check your bank or card statement for the charge.

| If the charge says... | You subscribed through | Cancel in |
|---|---|---|
| Apple.com/bill or App Store | Apple (iPhone or iPad) | iPhone Settings |
| Google Play or Google *ReelShort | Google Play (Android) | Google Play Store app |
| ReelShort or Crazy Maple Studio | Directly with ReelShort | The ReelShort app's VIP page, or ReelShort support |

*Statement wording varies by bank and country, so treat these as typical examples rather than exact text.*

If you're not sure, try the iPhone or Google Play steps first. If ReelShort doesn't appear in the list of subscriptions there, you're not being billed through that store.

## How to Cancel on iPhone or iPad

If you subscribed through the App Store, Apple handles the billing, so you cancel in your device settings:

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **your name** at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Find **ReelShort** in the list and tap it.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**, then confirm.

If you only see an option to choose a different plan and no cancel button, the subscription has most likely already been cancelled. Apple shows the date it will end instead.

## How to Cancel on Android

If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel in the Play Store:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your **profile picture** in the top right.
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **ReelShort**.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Google account you used to subscribe. If you have more than one account on the phone, the subscription may be under another one.

## How to Cancel Inside the ReelShort App

ReelShort's terms describe a third route that works from the app itself: log in to your account, go to the VIP page, and follow the Subscription Management instructions. In practice, tap the VIP membership banner in the app and scroll to the bottom to find the option to manage your subscription.

This route is useful if you're not sure which store you paid through, because it takes you to the right place. If you subscribed directly with ReelShort rather than through Apple or Google, it's the main way to cancel.

## The Billing-Date Rule Most People Miss

ReelShort's terms say you can cancel at any time, and that cancellation takes effect immediately for future billing periods. But there's a condition that catches people out. You must cancel **on or before the day before** your next billing date.

If you cancel on the billing date itself, you'll be charged for one more period. On a weekly plan that means one more week. On a yearly plan it means one more year.

So if your subscription renews on a Friday, cancel by Thursday at the latest. If you're not planning to keep the subscription, the safest habit is to cancel immediately after you subscribe. On both iPhone and Android, you normally keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for, so cancelling early rarely costs you anything.

## What Happens After You Cancel

Once you've cancelled, three things are worth knowing.

1. **You keep VIP until the paid period ends.** You won't lose access the moment you cancel. It runs out at the end of the week, month or year you've already paid for.
2. **Your coins stay in your account.** Cancelling a subscription doesn't affect coins you've bought or earned. You can keep spending them on episodes.
3. **ReelShort's reminder emails are infrequent.** According to its terms, ReelShort sends a renewal reminder by email once every six months. On a weekly plan, that's roughly 26 charges between reminders, so don't rely on the email to warn you.

## Can You Get a Refund?

ReelShort's own position is strict. Its terms say subscription payments are final and non-refundable except where the terms specify otherwise, and that purchases of coins and other virtual items are final too. If you're hoping for a refund, going to ReelShort directly is the least likely route to work.

If you subscribed on an iPhone, Apple runs its own refund process. According to [Apple's support page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223 "nofollow"), you sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com, choose "I'd like to," select "Request a refund," pick a reason and submit. Apple decides each request itself, and some purchases may be eligible.

If you subscribed through Google Play, [Google's refund policy page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en "nofollow") explains that refunds depend on what you bought, when and how you paid, and where you live. It also notes that most apps are made by third-party developers, who can process refunds under their own policies. In practice, that means trying both Google and ReelShort.

Whatever the route, act quickly. A request made soon after an unexpected charge is much easier to support than one made months later.

## If You Delete Your Account, Your Coins Are Gone

One detail in ReelShort's terms is easy to overlook. If you close your account, whether you delete it yourself or ask support to do it, all of your coins and other virtual items are forfeited. Unused coins aren't refunded.

If you still have a large coin balance, spend it before you delete anything. Or simply cancel the subscription and keep the account, since an account with no subscription costs nothing.

## If You Still Can't Cancel

Sometimes the subscription doesn't show up anywhere, or charges continue after you've cancelled. In that case, contact ReelShort support directly. According to [ReelShort's customer service guide](https://www.reelshort.com/fandom/reelshort-customer-service-01-25-3faa2218/ "nofollow"), you can reach the help center through the Feedback link at the bottom of the ReelShort website, or through your Profile and then Help/Feedback in the app.

To get a faster answer:

1. **Include your ReelShort User ID**, which is on your profile page in the app.
2. **Say which device you use**, whether it's an iPhone, Android phone or web browser.
3. **Attach a screenshot of the charge**, such as your App Store or Google Play receipt or your bank statement.

ReelShort says agents usually reply within 24 to 48 hours, and its support hours are listed as Monday to Sunday, 12am to 8am Eastern Time. You can also email reelshort.support@crazymaplestudio.com. If charges keep coming after all of that, contact your bank or card issuer and ask them to block future payments to ReelShort.

## Avoiding the Problem Next Time

The simplest way to never need this guide again is to avoid subscriptions you don't need. If you only want to finish one or two shows, buying coins is usually cheaper and there's nothing to cancel. Coins are a one-time purchase.

You can also try a show before spending anything. ShortsBinge hosts the free episodes of ReelShort series in your browser, with no account or payment. For example, [How to Tame a Silver Fox](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/how-to-tame-a-silver-fox-shicktsigdor/) has 12 free episodes. It's usually enough to know whether a series is worth paying for.

## FAQ

**Does deleting the ReelShort app cancel my subscription?**
No. The subscription is billed through Apple, Google or your card, and keeps renewing until you cancel it in your device settings, the Play Store or the app's VIP page.

**When should I cancel to avoid another charge?**
On or before the day before your next billing date. ReelShort's terms say that if you cancel on the billing date itself, you'll be charged for the next period.

**Will I lose VIP immediately after cancelling?**
No. You keep VIP access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

**Can I get a refund from ReelShort?**
ReelShort's terms say subscription payments are non-refundable. If you paid through Apple, you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you paid through Google Play, refunds depend on Google's policies and the developer.

**What happens to my coins if I delete my ReelShort account?**
They're forfeited. Spend them first, or cancel the subscription and keep the account instead.

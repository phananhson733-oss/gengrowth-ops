## 2023: The Werewolf Lead

### Fated to My Forbidden Alpha

Her breakout came in June 2023 with [Fated to My Forbidden Alpha](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/fated-to-my-forbidden-alpha-bqesedttthls/), one of ReelShort's earlier werewolf dramas. She plays Selene Lovette. On her birthday, Selene discovers that her fated mate is her nemesis, Alpha Jackson, who plans to reject her. While escaping her pack she meets Alexander, her true fated mate.

It has all the elements that werewolf shorts still rely on: the birthday reveal, the rejected mate, a hidden identity and a second, better match. IMDb credits her in 53 episodes, and its 399 votes make it one of her most-rated titles. Its rating, 6.7, is the lowest among her lead roles here, so it's better watched as a look at where she started than as her best work. It's 60 episodes long, with 8 free.

### Married at First Sight

Five months later she was the lead in a very different story. [Married at First Sight](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/married-at-first-sight-pm6n3k542ble/), released in November 2023, is a billionaire contract-marriage romance. She plays Summer, who has given everything to a boyfriend who then cheats on her. She enters a contract marriage with a billionaire stranger who promised a divorce, and he then refuses to let her go.

This is the most-rated of her dramas on IMDb, with 427 votes and a 7.3 average. IMDb credits her in all 89 episodes, which makes it her longest lead on the list. If you want to see why ReelShort kept casting her, it's a good place to start, since the whole show rests on her. Only 6 episodes are free, though, so you'll know quickly whether it's for you, but you'll hit the paywall early too.

## 2024: Billionaires and Babies

### His One and Only Love

Her 2024 lead in the ShortsBinge library is [His One and Only Love](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/his-one-and-only-love-4css45s5zhra/), opposite Jesse Morales. She plays Lyla, who is worn down by an abusive family and makes a wish for true love on her birthday. She then saves a gentle CEO and ends up pregnant.

IMDb's own summary adds a detail the short synopsis leaves out: Lyla is a poor casino waitress, and when the billionaire finds out about the baby, he marries and spoils her and punishes anyone who wronged her. The ShortsBinge tags are an honest guide to the tone, from Age Gap and Flash Marriage to Hot Daddy/DILF and Protective Husband. It's rated 7.7 from 192 votes, the second-best score among her titles here, and 11 of its 80 episodes are free.

## 2025: Her Busiest Year

2025 is the year her IMDb list gets crowded. She has more than a dozen credits dated 2025, and four of them are on ShortsBinge. They show two sides of her work: the leads she carries, and the supporting parts she fits in between.

### Back to the Pack: In Love with the Alpha

In January 2025 she returned to werewolf fantasy with [In Love with the Alpha](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/in-love-with-the-alpha-q4hgbwahlkjg/). She plays Maya Woods. When Maya gets her wolf, she learns that the pack Alpha, Liam, is her fated mate, and that a Rogue King wants her as the True Luna who will complete his power.

Compared with her 2023 werewolf role, the stakes are larger. Maya isn't just a rejected mate trying to survive. She's the key to someone else's dark plan. It's rated 7.1 from 163 votes, with 6 of its 72 episodes free.

### Two Pregnancy Leads

[Pregnant and Spoiled by the Billionaire](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/pregnant-and-spoiled-by-the-billionaire-usybwyalpqvk/), from March 2025, casts her as Amelia, a hard-working college junior. IMDb's summary explains how her night goes wrong: a roommate gets her a bar job, then drugs her and tries to traffic her. The billionaire Leo rescues her, one night together leaves her pregnant, and he assumes she's only after his money. It's rated 7.2 from 129 votes. At 56 episodes it's the shortest series on this list, and 11 of them are free.

[Carrying His Triplets, Becoming His Wifey](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/carrying-his-triplets-becoming-his-wifey-vyqn5q3zsmuq/), released in September 2025, pushes the same idea further. She plays Daisy, a college student working a warehouse job to cover tuition until her father takes her savings. She crosses paths with the billionaire Marcus and soon learns she's expecting triplets. It's rated 7.3 from 244 votes. It also has the longest free run of all her dramas here, 12 of 69 episodes, which makes it the cheapest way to spend an evening with one of her leads.

One oddity is worth flagging if you're checking her filmography. IMDb currently credits her in only one episode of Carrying His Triplets, even though ShortsBinge lists her first in the cast and the heroine is Daisy, the role IMDb gives her. Episode-level credits on IMDb are often incomplete for vertical dramas, so don't read too much into the number.

### A Supporting Turn: Open Your Eyes, My Billionaire Husband

Not every 2025 role was a lead. In [Open Your Eyes, My Billionaire Husband](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/open-your-eyes-my-billionaire-husband-2o3edg4p2g4b/), a woman who can't pay her mother's medical bills agrees to marry a comatose billionaire, who then wakes up. IMDb credits Autumn as Hailey Rockwell Trent, and she appears third in the ShortsBinge cast list, after Seth Edeen and Joss Gyorkos.

So if you start this one expecting her to lead it, you'll be surprised. It's still worth a look if you're working through her filmography: it's rated 7.2 from 211 votes. But with just 4 free episodes out of 72, it's the smallest free run of her dramas here.

## 2026: Her Best-Reviewed Role

### The Words

If you only watch one Autumn Noel drama, make it [The Words](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-words-qox5dlprqlgk/). Released in January 2026, it's her highest-rated title on IMDb, 8.0 from 289 votes, and it's unlike anything else on this list.

She plays Lennon. Years ago, the rocker Phoenix broke her heart and ran off with her song. IMDb's summary fills in the rest: Lennon was the nerdy good girl, Phoenix the bad boy who stole her song to escape his abusive father. Now he's famous and spiraling into drugs and alcohol, and she's hired to keep him clean on tour.

There's no billionaire, no pregnancy and no werewolf. The story runs on music, addiction and an old betrayal, with the tags Enemies to Lovers, Love-Hate Reunion and Celebrity. In most of her other leads, a powerful man steps in to save her character. Here she's the one sent to save him. The rating suggests viewers noticed. Its 67 episodes include 9 free ones.

### The Retitled Werewolf Drama: My Alpha, My Forbidden Fate

The other 2026 entry comes with a twist. [My Alpha, My Forbidden Fate](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/my-alpha-my-forbidden-fate-pneuxfajipok/) was released in July 2026, and it's closely tied to her 2023 debut.

The cast is the same, with Kasey Esser, John Williams Dicario, Autumn Noel and Antheia. The length is the same, 60 episodes with 8 free. The premise matches too. ReelShort's one-line plot says Selena is rejected by her partner before the Moon Goddess steps in, which is the same rejected-mate setup as the 2023 show. Even the heroine's name has only changed by one letter, from Selene to Selena.

We couldn't find ReelShort saying outright that it's a re-release, and IMDb doesn't list it separately, so we won't claim more than the evidence shows. But if you've already watched Fated to My Forbidden Alpha, expect to recognize this one.

## Before ReelShort

Short dramas weren't her start. Her IMDb biography says she moved to Los Angeles at 19 to pursue acting and first gained attention in Lifetime movies. Her credits bear that out. Her first TV-movie role was Sophie in the 2021 film *A Dangerous Defense*, followed by *Deadly Garage Sale* (2022), and she did guest work on the YouTube series *Chicken Girls* and several Dhar Mann episodes.

She has also started talking about the industry she works in. IMDb lists her as an interviewee on the vertical-drama podcasts *ReelTalk* and *The Vertical Take*, both in 2025. And she's credited as a writer on a 2020 podcast episode, a small sign of interests beyond acting.

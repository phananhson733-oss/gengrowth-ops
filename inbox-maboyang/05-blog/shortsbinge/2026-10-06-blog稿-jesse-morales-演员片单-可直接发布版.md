If you've watched a ReelShort age-gap romance in the past two years, there's a good chance Jesse Morales was the older man at the center of it. He's the silver fox in How to Tame a Silver Fox, the professor in Pregnant by My Ex's Professor Dad and the ex's father in The Imperfect Marriage. He has become a familiar face of what ShortsBinge tags as "Hot Daddy" romance.

His own IMDb biography describes the type he's known for: strong, protective, emotionally controlled men. It fits most of his work, but not all of it. This guide sorts his dramas by the kind of man he plays, points out where he breaks the pattern, and covers how a former professional dancer ended up leading vertical dramas. It sticks to his roles and career.

## Before Vertical Dramas: A Decade as a Dancer

Morales didn't start as an actor. According to his IMDb biography, he spent about ten years as a professional dancer. He was a featured performer in Magic Mike Live, appeared on The Ellen DeGeneres Show, and danced alongside artists including Beyoncé and Mariah Carey. His earliest screen credits reflect that: a Magic Mike dancer in a 2018 episode of This Is Us, and a 2015 promotional video of Channing Tatum crashing a Magic Mike XXL screening.

His profile also notes that he worked as a tennis coach while pursuing acting, and that he still plays tennis and golf. That detail matters more than it sounds, because two of his recent dramas are built around exactly those sports.

His first vertical-drama leads arrived in 2024, and the pace since has been remarkable. ReelShort's own [actor page for him](https://www.reelshort.com/tags/movie-actors/jesse-morales-movies-676d21074582b53a1408166f "nofollow") counts 34 ReelShort dramas, of which 13 are currently on ShortsBinge. By 2026 he had moved from star to judge. IMDb lists him as a mentor and judge on The Next ReelStar, ReelShort's reality competition in which men compete for a breakout lead role.

## Type One: The Silver Fox

This is the role that made his name, and the one most viewers search for. In each of these, he plays an older, powerful man who is off-limits for a reason, and the drama comes from that line being crossed.

[How to Tame a Silver Fox](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/how-to-tame-a-silver-fox-shicktsigdor/) is his most-rated drama on IMDb by a wide margin, with 953 votes and a 7.1 average. He plays Chris Collins, the business partner of Harper Reeves's father. Harper, a Yale senior, plans to seduce Chris so her father will throw him out, but every time he rescues her from trouble, she falls a little harder. It has 12 free episodes out of 71.

[The Imperfect Marriage](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-imperfect-marriage-mefcub4vvqpm/) raises the stakes. After her fiancé cheats, Vivian rushes into a marriage deal with a silver fox, and then discovers that her new husband, Quentin, is her ex's father. Their arrangement is simple: two weeks of marriage, a CEO title for Quentin, and enough money for Vivian to save her sick dad. It's rated 7.6 and has 16 free episodes, one of the longest free runs among his dramas.

[Pregnant by My Ex's Professor Dad](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/pregnant-by-my-ex-s-professor-dad-k52ge5hvizek/) adds a classroom. After catching her boyfriend cheating, Emily has a one-night stand with a stranger who turns out to be her new professor, Charles Hayes, and she later learns she's pregnant. It's rated 7.4 from 320 votes, his second most-rated title, though it offers only 4 free episodes.

[The Billionaire and the Baby Trap](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-billionaire-and-the-baby-trap-3qowopsvh4ep/) completes the set. Elara needs a baby to escape a loveless marriage, and the man fate puts in her path is Cole, a hidden billionaire who is also her husband's uncle. At 7.8 it's one of his best-rated dramas, though it has fewer votes so far.

## Type Two: The Contract Billionaire

Before the silver fox roles took over, Morales spent much of 2024 and early 2025 as the billionaire in contract and fake-marriage romances. These are gentler than the taboo dramas above, and a good place to start if the age-gap stories aren't for you.

[Baby Trapped by the Billionaire](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/baby-trapped-by-the-billionaire-lqkworwbn7xl/) is the best-rated of this group, at 7.8 from 247 votes. With her sister's cancer getting worse, Katie agrees to carry a billionaire's heir under a contract with one condition: nobody falls in love. He plays Evan Brandt, the billionaire who breaks that rule first.

[Fake Married to My Billionaire CEO](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/fake-married-to-my-billionaire-ceo-cjogvrrxh5ot/) is his longest drama on ShortsBinge, at 93 episodes. Betrayed and robbed by her ex, a woman marries a stranger who turns out to be hiding an enormous fortune. He plays that stranger, Calladan Vandalay.

[Mr Hills' Adorable Wife](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/mr-hills-adorable-wife-hskr4osuc7om/) puts him opposite Meg Bush, who also appears with him in Baby Trapped by the Billionaire. Overlooked by her own family because of a birthmark, Valerie wins the heart of billionaire Andrew Hills, only for her family to nearly swap her sister into her place.

[His One and Only Love](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/his-one-and-only-love-4css45s5zhra/) is the sweetest of the four. Worn down by an abusive family, Lyla, played by Autumn Noel, wishes for true love on her birthday, then saves a gentle CEO and ends up pregnant. He plays Leo, and IMDb's summary describes him marrying and spoiling her and punishing anyone who comes after her. It's rated 7.7.

## Type Three: The Man You're Meant to Hate

Every so often he plays against type, and the ratings show how hard that is in a genre built on devotion.

[My Firefighter Ex-Husband Burns in Regret](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/my-firefighter-ex-husband-burns-in-regret-duaztpo3ftsq/) is the clearest example. He plays Jace, a firefighter who, when a youth center collapses, rescues his ex-lover and her daughter instead of his own daughter, Alyssa. His grieving wife, Hazel, sets out to make him pay. It's rated 6.5, his lowest alongside one other title. Regret stories only work if viewers want the man to suffer, so a low score for the character doesn't necessarily reflect the performance.

## Type Four: Outside the Romance Box

A handful of his roles don't fit any of the patterns above.

[I Don't Need No Alpha Brothers](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/i-don-t-need-no-alpha-brothers-weskhpgc6xlh/) is his first werewolf drama, released in September 2026. He plays Alpha Mason Turner, the pack leader whose life Elara saved as a child. She's adopted into the Stormfang Pack and expected to marry one of his five sons, until another girl's lies turn all of them against her. It has 14 free episodes.

[Sweet Temptation](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/sweet-temptation-jzdpoh6kpvy6/) moves him into mafia romance. Giulia is promised against her will to a feared crime lord and must take his late wife's place, as a bride and as a mother to his two children. ShortsBinge lists him second in the cast after Talia Wynzel, and IMDb credits him as Cassio Moretti. It's rated 7.7 from 121 votes.

[American Sniper: The Last Round](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/american-sniper-the-last-round-cz6oa5kk52y7/) is an action drama in which he has a supporting part. A record-breaking marksman hides as a maintenance worker at a shooting range until a hostile takeover forces him to reveal what he can do. IMDb credits Morales as Chris Redford, and the sniper himself is another character.

[Meet My Brothers](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/meet-my-brothers-loplwtpxebge/) is another ensemble drama. Divorced, pregnant and cornered by her ex-husband's mistress, Catherine Rowen learns she's the missing daughter of a wealthy family with three brothers. IMDb lists the same story as After Divorce, I'm Spoiled by Three Brothers and credits him as Timothy, so expect a smaller role here too.

## Roles Worth Knowing That Aren't on ShortsBinge Yet

Two of his best-rated recent dramas connect directly to the sports he plays off screen. In Queen of the Court, rated 8.0 from 240 votes, a former tennis star coaches her boyfriend to a Grand Slam, only to face his affair with a rising player. In Ace! The Golf King's Reconquest, he's credited as "Lion," the nickname of a former golf champion who vanished at his peak and now returns to the game to pay for his daughter's tuition.

He also appears in Dominated by My Dad's Boss (7.7), Rented a Billionaire Husband for Christmas (7.8) and The Cooking Queen: A Recipe for Divorce (7.9). If any of these arrive in the library, this list will grow.

If you want the role he's best known for, start with How to Tame a Silver Fox.

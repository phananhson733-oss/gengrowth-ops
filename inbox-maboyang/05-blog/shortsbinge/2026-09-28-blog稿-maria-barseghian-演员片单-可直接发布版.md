## Start Here If You've Never Seen Her

If you only try one, go with [Breaking the Deal with My Hockey Bad Boy](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/breaking-the-deal-with-my-hockey-bad-boy-bp6c7trxqynm/). It carries the longest free run of any title on this list, 17 episodes, so you can get well past the setup before deciding whether you're hooked. Maria plays Jane, who has a crush on her stepbrother Nate. When Nate's girlfriend Melissa threatens to expose it, Jane's old enemy Zach Gates offers to fake-date her as cover, and the pretending doesn't stay pretend for long.

If you'd rather start with the highest-rated title, that's [In Love With My Brother's Best Friend](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/i-m-in-love-with-my-brother-s-best-friend-ccarl5npgp7e/), tied at 7.8 with the most votes behind it. She plays Kaitlyn, a college freshman who moves in with her brother after catching her boyfriend cheating and finds herself falling for his best friend, Cole. The obstacle here is less about the two of them and more about what her brother will think.

## When the Relationship Starts as an Act

Fake dating is the premise she has returned to most. Beyond the hockey drama above, there's [Faking It with My Ex's Best Friend](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/faking-it-with-my-ex-s-best-friend-smcgwtfwg4hl/), her newest title here. Clara fakes amnesia to test her boyfriend, catches him cheating, and then starts fake-dating his best friend Ethan to make him jealous.

The two share a setup but differ in motive. Jane's fake relationship is protection: it exists to keep a secret buried. Clara's is revenge: it exists to make someone else feel it. If you like one, the other is the obvious follow-up, and the contrast between them is part of the fun.

## Growing Up Is Complicated Enough Without Family Getting in the Way

Two of her dramas put a family arrangement right in the middle of a school romance. In [Hating and Loving My Adopted Brother](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/hating-and-loving-my-adopted-brother-daalzmwblpge/), Abigail loses both parents in an accident and is taken in by her dad's friend. She transfers schools, clashes with a classmate named Chris, and then learns he's her new adopted brother.

[The Virgin's Bucket List](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-virgin-s-bucket-list-fxsix4kjdorz/) is lighter on its feet. Lindsay wants a boyfriend before graduation and falls for the football recruit living under her father's roof. The secret relationship is only half the story, though. The site's synopsis gives the fight against bullying at school equal weight, which makes it a good pick if you want a romance with a bit of backbone. It's also her longest drama at 82 episodes, with 13 free.

## When the Man Pursuing Her Is the Danger

Three of her dramas pair her with a powerful man whose intentions are the central question.

In [In the Palm of His Hand](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/in-the-palm-of-his-hand-6lsuhlplikg3/), Shelby Yates is a college senior who accidentally saves the life of Matteo Franconi, a billionaire CEO with a mafia past. He decides on the spot that he wants to marry her, and the drama keeps asking whether he's as dangerous as he seems.

[Ruthless Mafia Daddy](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/ruthless-mafia-daddy-kela3tjveeec/) starts with a double blow. On the day Ellie's IVF succeeds, she learns her fiancé and her best friend are having an affair. A clinic mix-up means the baby's father is a mafia boss, and she ends up in a contract marriage in the middle of a succession war. Only five episodes are free here, the shortest run on this list, so expect to reach the cliffhanger quickly.

High Society moves the same tension into a period setting. Adele, an orphan off the streets, agrees to pose as Duke Cesare Buonaparte's long-lost sister so she can marry into the Della Valle family in his place. We covered it in more depth in our [High Society guide](https://shortsbinge.com/best-short-dramas/high-society/), including the one rule their deal is built on.

## Her Werewolf Dramas

If you're here for fated mates, she has two, and they sit at opposite ends of her career on this list.

[Fated to the Alpha](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/fated-to-the-alpha-zwgbyx6erw4y/) is her earliest title here, released in May 2024, and it has by far the most IMDb votes of any of them. She plays Kat Hartley, a high schooler her pack scapegoats and rejects because her wolf hasn't arrived. She joins a new pack and falls for her alpha superior, whose uncle wants her dead.

[The Cursed Alpha's Mate](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-cursed-alpha-s-mate-fpm2fh7xyzua/) takes a very different route in. Shay Santos catches her boyfriend Dean cheating, vows to go home with the next man who approaches her, and that man turns out to be Mal Haywood, a cursed Alpha.

If you want more of this side of the catalog, the site's [werewolf collection](https://shortsbinge.com/browse/identities/werewolf/) is the place to keep going.

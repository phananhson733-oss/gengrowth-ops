# Is ReelShort Legit? Is DramaBox Safe? What to Know Before You Pay

**Meta description:** ReelShort and DramaBox are real, licensed apps from established companies, but their subscriptions trip people up. Who runs them, what users complain about, and how to stay safe.

---

If you've been pulled into a vertical drama from a TikTok clip, the next question is usually whether the app behind it is worth trusting with your card. "Is ReelShort legit" and "is DramaBox free" are some of the most common searches about these platforms, and the honest answer has two parts.

Both apps are legitimate products from real, well-funded companies, available in the official app stores, and neither is a scam in the sense of stealing your money for nothing. But both are built to get you paying, and a large share of the complaints about them come from one specific thing: subscriptions that renew before people realize it.

This guide covers who is behind each app, what real users say, where people get caught out, and how to watch without surprises.

That's normal for this industry. Vertical short dramas started in China, and the biggest international players grew out of that market.

## How Big They Actually Are

Scale doesn't prove an app is good, but it does make "is this a scam?" an easy question to rule out.

- **ReelShort** reportedly passed 370 million downloads and around $700 million in revenue in 2025, according to figures cited on its Wikipedia page.
- **DramaBox** passed 100 million downloads by August 2025 and reported $323 million in revenue for 2024. In July 2025 it was selected for the Disney Accelerator program.

Both apps show 100 million+ downloads on Google Play. These aren't small apps that might vanish with your coin balance next month.

The two sources measure different things. Google Play reviews come mostly from people using the app day to day, and millions of them are broadly positive about the shows themselves. Trustpilot tends to attract people with a grievance, and neither company has claimed its profile there. DramaBox's Trustpilot score is based on only six reviews, which is far too few to mean much.

Still, ReelShort's Trustpilot reviews are useful precisely because they're consistent. Trustpilot's own summary of recent reviews highlights the same few complaints: charges that keep coming after people thought they'd stopped paying, difficulty cancelling subscriptions, slow customer support, and the feeling that the app is more expensive than its "free" framing suggests.

One more thing to watch: sites with names similar to the official apps have their own separate review pages. Make sure any review you read is actually about the app you're using.

## Where People Actually Get Caught: Subscriptions

Almost every serious complaint traces back to how these apps charge. There are two ways to pay:

1. **Coins**, bought in packs and spent per episode. On ReelShort a standard locked episode usually costs 60 coins, and the smallest pack is 500 coins for $4.99.
2. **VIP subscriptions**, which unlock episodes without coins and remove ads. ReelShort offers weekly, monthly and yearly VIP plans, priced from $19.99 to $199 depending on the plan and region.

Coins are pay-as-you-go: you buy a pack and it's done. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them, and a weekly plan renews every seven days. That's where the "unexpected charges" in the reviews usually come from. People subscribe to finish one series, forget about it, and keep paying week after week.

None of this is hidden in the sense of being illegal. It's the standard subscription model used across app stores. But it's easy to trigger with one tap in the middle of a cliffhanger, which is exactly when these apps ask you to pay.

## How to Cancel a Subscription Properly

The most important thing to know: **deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.** Subscriptions bought through your phone are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app.

**On iPhone or iPad:** open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose ReelShort or DramaBox and tap Cancel Subscription.

**On Android:** open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, go to Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Choose the app and tap Cancel.

After cancelling, you'll usually keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. If you subscribed on a website rather than through an app store, you'll need to cancel from your account on that website instead.

If you've been charged for a renewal you didn't intend, refunds for app store purchases are requested through Apple or Google under their own policies. Contact the store rather than only the app's support team. Whether a refund is granted is up to them, so act quickly and don't count on it.

## Is It Safe to Download?

Yes, as long as you download from the official App Store or Google Play listing. The apps themselves are mainstream products with over 100 million installs each.

The real risks come from unofficial shortcuts. "Mod APK" versions promising free coins and "coin generator" sites are not legitimate. ReelShort itself warns that they can steal your login or payment details, install malware, and get your account permanently banned. If a site asks you to download a modified app or fill in a survey for free coins, close it.

## Is It Free?

Partly. Both apps are free to download, and every series opens with a run of free episodes, typically somewhere between 5 and 17 on the titles we carry. After that, episodes are unlocked with coins or a subscription. Both apps also hand out free coins through daily check-ins, rewarded ads and in-app tasks, which let you keep going slowly without paying.

So "free" is accurate for the first episodes and for a patient viewer who collects daily rewards. It isn't accurate if you expect to binge a full 60-episode series in one night without spending anything.

## A Quick Checklist Before You Pay

1. **Try the free episodes first**, in the app or in your browser, and only pay for a series you actually want to finish.
2. **Prefer coins over a subscription** unless you watch several series a week.
3. **If you do subscribe, set a reminder** for the renewal date the moment you sign up.
4. **Cancel through Apple or Google**, not by deleting the app.
5. **Only install from official app stores**, and ignore any "free coins" hack.

## FAQ

**Is ReelShort legit?**
Yes. ReelShort is a real app made by Crazy Maple Studio, which is partly owned by Beijing-based COL Group. It has more than 100 million downloads on Google Play. Most complaints are about subscription charges and cancellations, not fraud.

**Is DramaBox safe?**
Yes, when downloaded from the official App Store or Google Play. It's run by Singapore-based StoryMatrix, a subsidiary of Beijing's Dianzhong Technology, and has more than 100 million downloads.

**Is ReelShort a Chinese app?**
ReelShort is made by Crazy Maple Studio, which is backed and partly owned by COL Group, a Beijing-based digital publisher. DramaBox is also owned by a Beijing-based company.

**Is DramaBox free?**
The app is free to download and every series has free episodes. After those, you unlock episodes with coins or a subscription, or earn coins for free through daily rewards and ads.

**Why was I charged again after I stopped watching?**
Most likely you have an active subscription, which renews automatically. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in your iPhone settings or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

**Can I get a refund from ReelShort or DramaBox?**
For purchases made through the App Store or Google Play, refund requests go through Apple or Google under their policies. Refunds aren't guaranteed, so it's best to cancel before the renewal date.

**Are ReelShort mod APKs safe?**
No. ReelShort warns that modified apps and coin generators can steal your data, infect your phone and get your account banned.

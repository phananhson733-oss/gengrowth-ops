# How to Cancel a DramaBox Subscription: iPhone, Android, Website and Guest Accounts

**Meta description:** Cancel DramaBox membership on iPhone, Android or the website, including if you paid as a guest. What each plan costs while you wait, DramaBox's refund rules, and what to do if the charges won't stop.

---

DramaBox's weekly membership is the option most people end up on, and it's also the one most people end up trying to cancel. On the website it's the pre-selected plan, labeled "97% of users pick this," and after two introductory weeks it renews at $19.99 every week until you stop it.

Cancelling takes a minute or two once you know where your subscription lives. This guide covers every route: iPhone, Android, the website, and the tricky case of paying as a guest without an account. It also explains what DramaBox's own terms say about refunds.

*Prices are from DramaBox's website top-up page (US), checked on September 30, 2026. Policy details come from [DramaBox's Terms of Use](https://support.dramaboxdb.com/terms.html "nofollow") and Apple's and Google's official help pages.*

## First, Check What Staying Subscribed Costs You

Before you cancel, it helps to know which plan you're on and how fast it adds up. These are the two memberships on DramaBox's website:

| Plan | Price | Cost if you forget to cancel |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | $15.99/week for 2 weeks, then $19.99/week | About $72 after one month, $152 after eight weeks, over $1,000 after a year |
| Yearly | $149.99/year | $149.99 again on the renewal date |

Both plans renew automatically. The weekly plan is the one to deal with urgently: eight weeks of it already costs more than a full year of the yearly plan. If you're on the yearly plan, you have more time, but put the renewal date in your calendar.

## The Two Mistakes That Keep the Charges Coming

Most "I cancelled but DramaBox still charged me" stories come down to one of two things.

1. **Deleting the app.** Removing DramaBox from your phone doesn't touch your subscription. The payment is held by Apple, Google or your card, and it keeps renewing.
2. **Cancelling in the wrong place.** If you subscribed through the App Store, there's nothing to cancel in Google Play, and vice versa. If you paid on the website, neither store will show it. You have to cancel where you paid.

So start by checking your bank or card statement for the charge. It will usually show whether it came through Apple, Google or a direct card payment.

## How to Cancel DramaBox on iPhone or iPad

If you subscribed in the app on an Apple device, Apple handles the billing:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **your name** at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **DramaBox**.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

If you don't see a cancel button, only an option to choose a plan, the subscription has probably already been cancelled, and Apple will show the date it ends.

## How to Cancel DramaBox on Android

If you subscribed through Google Play:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your **profile picture** in the top right.
3. Go to **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **DramaBox**.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.

If DramaBox doesn't appear, check that you're signed in to the right Google account. In a Google Help thread about exactly this problem, the community advice was blunt: Google can't cancel a subscription for you, so make completely sure you're in the account that paid.

## Cancelling from Inside the App

A widely shared Reddit answer from a couple of years ago describes a route inside the DramaBox app itself: go to your **Profile** in the bottom right, tap the **gear icon** in the top right, then tap **Manage Subscription** at the top and **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom. App layouts change, so the exact labels may differ, but the subscription settings are generally under your profile.

For app-store subscriptions, this route usually hands you over to Apple's or Google's subscription page anyway, so the device steps above are the most reliable.

## How to Cancel If You Paid on the DramaBox Website

DramaBox's website sells memberships directly, paid with Google Pay or a card, and these don't appear in your phone's subscription settings.

The website's own top-up page explains where to manage them. According to its tips, you click your **profile picture in the top right corner** to open your personal center, where you can manage your subscriptions. For other problems, it points you to customer service through **Profile, then Help & feedback**.

If you paid by card on the website and can't find the subscription, contact DramaBox support through Help & feedback with the date, amount and last four digits of the card. If that doesn't work, your card issuer can block future payments to DramaBox.

## What If You Paid as a Guest?

This is one of the most common searches about DramaBox, and for good reason. The website lets you top up as a **Guest User** without logging in. The checkout page shows a guest ID number and a prompt to "Log in to top up," but the payment buttons are available without signing in.

The problem is that a guest purchase isn't tied to a login you can return to. A few things improve your chances:

1. **Use the same browser and device** you paid on. The guest session may still be there, giving you access to the profile menu.
2. **Find your guest ID.** It appears at the top of the top-up page. Include it in any message to support.
3. **Keep your payment receipt** from Google Pay or your card, with the date and amount.
4. **Contact support** through Help & feedback with all of the above.

If you can't get through to DramaBox, your card issuer or Google Pay can stop future charges. The best fix, though, is preventive: log in to an account before you pay for anything.

## Did You Subscribe to DramaBox Lite?

DramaBox's publisher, StoryMatrix, also releases a separate app called **DramaBox Lite**. If you can't find DramaBox in your subscriptions list, look for DramaBox Lite instead. It's a different app, and its subscription has to be cancelled separately.

## Can You Get a Refund?

DramaBox's position is short and clear. Section 7.1 of its Terms of Use says all fees "are in U.S. dollars and are non-refundable." The same section says coins can only be used in DramaBox and can't be exchanged for any currency.

The terms also note that payments are processed by third parties such as Apple and Google Play, and those companies run their own refund processes.

For iPhone purchases, [Apple's refund page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223 "nofollow") explains the steps: sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com, choose "I'd like to," select "Request a refund," and give a reason. Apple decides each case itself.

For Android purchases, [Google's refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en "nofollow") says refunds depend on what you bought, when, and where you live, and that developers can also issue refunds under their own policies. Request one through Google Play, and contact DramaBox as well.

Whichever route you use, act soon after the unexpected charge. A quick request is much easier to support than one made months later.

## What Happens to Your Coins and Account

Cancelling a membership doesn't delete your account or your coins. You can keep watching with coins and free rewards.

Deleting your account is different. DramaBox's terms say that once you delete it, you lose the right to access all or part of the platform. If you have coins left, use them before you delete anything, or just cancel the subscription and keep the account.

## A Cheaper Way to Watch From Now On

DramaBox itself steers users toward memberships. In replies to reviews on its app listing, it has said it recommends a subscription rather than coins, because a subscription unlocks every drama. That's true, but it only pays off if you watch a lot. If you subscribed just to finish one show, you probably didn't need a subscription at all. On DramaBox's website, coin packs start at 500 coins for $4.99, and there's nothing to cancel afterwards.

You can also try a DramaBox series before paying anything. ShortsBinge hosts the free episodes of DramaBox shows in your browser, with no account or payment. For example, [SSS-Rank: The Slum-Born Thunder God](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/sss-rank-the-slum-born-thunder-god-a5loatfdn2kv/) has 5 free episodes.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my DramaBox subscription?**
Cancel it where you paid: iPhone Settings, then your name and Subscriptions; Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions; or, for website purchases, your profile picture on the DramaBox website.

**Does deleting the DramaBox app cancel my subscription?**
No. The subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it in Apple, Google Play or your DramaBox web account.

**How do I cancel DramaBox if I paid as a guest?**
Use the same browser you paid on, note your guest ID from the top-up page, and contact DramaBox through Help & feedback with your receipt. If that fails, ask your card issuer or Google Pay to stop future payments.

**Can I get a refund from DramaBox?**
DramaBox's terms say all fees are non-refundable. For App Store purchases you can request a refund from Apple, and for Google Play purchases through Google.

**How much does DramaBox charge per week?**
On the website, the weekly membership costs $15.99 for the first two weeks, then $19.99 per week.

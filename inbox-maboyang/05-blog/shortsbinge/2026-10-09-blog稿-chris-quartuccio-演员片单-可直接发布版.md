## Who Is Chris Quartuccio?

Chris Quartuccio is an American actor from Long Island, New York. According to his IMDb biography, he started on stage, making his Off-Broadway debut in 2018 as Daniel Beauxhomme in Once On This Island at the Helen Mills Theater in New York City.

His early screen credits are small parts in bigger productions. He played a bellhop in the 2019 Lifetime holiday movie Christmas Reservations and Prince Boris in the 2020 fantasy film Anastasia: Once Upon a Time. His biography also mentions an appearance in Amazon's Modern Love and a role as Young Tyler in the Neon Trees music video for "Feel Good."

His vertical-drama career began in 2024, and it moved quickly. IMDb now lists more than 20 short-drama credits for him, and he has two more projects, The Good Dark and Haven, in post-production.

## What Is His Best-Known Role?

By IMDb votes, it's [The Quarterback Next Door](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/the-quarterback-next-door-2ovo5xmmspoj/), with 277 votes and a 7.8 rating, his most-rated drama on ShortsBinge. He plays Jamie Donner, the most popular guy in school and the heroine's lifelong neighbor. A wallflower senior's crush on him goes public, and then a house fire moves him in with her family.

It's the role that defines his type: the popular athlete who turns out to be kinder than his reputation. At 87 episodes, it's also one of his longest shows, with 9 free on ShortsBinge.

## Does He Play Athletes a Lot?

Yes, and it's become his signature. Besides the quarterback, he plays a hockey player in [Breaking the Deal with My Hockey Bad Boy](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/breaking-the-deal-with-my-hockey-bad-boy-bp6c7trxqynm/). He's Zach Gates, who offers to be Jane's fake boyfriend to keep her secret from her old bully. IMDb's summary fills in the secret: Jane has a crush on her stepbrother, and Zach is the one who finds out.

It's rated 7.7 from 239 votes, and it has the longest free run of any of his dramas, 17 episodes out of 67. Maria Barseghian appears in this one too, making it the second of their dramas together.

His IMDb list continues the theme beyond ShortsBinge. He's Mason Clark in My Ex's Brother Teaches Me to Puck, another hockey romance, from 2026.

## Has He Played a Billionaire?

He has, though less often than many ReelShort leads. [Sweet Wild Bride of Mine](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/sweet-wild-bride-of-mine-nqq4judsuroy/) is the standout. Self-made billionaire Eliza survives a murder attempt, wakes with amnesia, and marries the stranger in a wheelchair who saved her, without knowing he's a billionaire faking his disability. IMDb credits him as that stranger, Justin Patton. It's rated 7.7 from 190 votes.

[My Double Proxy Marriage to a Billionaire](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/my-double-proxy-marriage-to-a-billionaire-76vqa7a32zwy/) is a lighter take. Sent to sign papers for a match her father arranged, Ella discovers she's already married, to a CEO who refuses to believe it. He plays Cole, the skeptical CEO whose grandmother adores Ella. It's one of his newest titles, from April 2026.

Two of his titles sit well below the rest on IMDb, and both are heavier, more dramatic stories than his campus romances.

He plays Thomas Cohen. It's his lowest-rated title, at 5.7.

## Is He in a Werewolf Drama?

Yes, as of September 2026. In [I Don't Need No Alpha Brothers](https://shortsbinge.com/drama/i-don-t-need-no-alpha-brothers-weskhpgc6xlh/) he plays Gabriel Turner, one of the five sons of Alpha Mason. The same drama features Jesse Morales as Alpha Mason, so it's a rare chance to see two of ReelShort's regular leads in one show. It has 14 free episodes.

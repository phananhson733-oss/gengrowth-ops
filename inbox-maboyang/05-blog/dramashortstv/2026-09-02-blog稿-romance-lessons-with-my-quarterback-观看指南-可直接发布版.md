## Why the Tutoring Deal Works as a Setup

Forced-proximity plots are a genre staple for a reason: put two people who already have unresolved tension in a room together on a recurring basis, and the story doesn't need a big coincidence to keep pushing them closer. A tutoring arrangement does that naturally, with a built-in excuse to meet again and again.

What makes this version work is that both sides think they're getting something else out of it. Jaxon is playing a long game to stay near Leo; Leo thinks he's working toward a completely different relationship. That mismatch is where most of the tension comes from before either of them says what they actually want.

If you've watched other enemies-to-lovers dramas, you already know the shape of this one. The pull here is watching exactly which moment breaks the arrangement open.

*Meta description: Evan Adams is the ReelShort actor behind teen romance hits like The Senator's Son and Summer Situationship. Age, height, and full drama list — not to be confused with the Canadian actor of the same name.*

## From Modeling to Campus Romance Lead

Before ReelShort, Evan Adams built a modeling career and picked up early acting credits including the indie film *Reed's Point* (2022). He's since become a go-to lead for ReelShort's youth and campus-romance dramas, typically cast as the charismatic "bad boy" love interest. He's also expanded beyond ReelShort — he's appeared in short dramas on DramaBox as well, making him one of the few actors actively working across multiple short-drama platforms rather than exclusive to one app.

## Evan Adams' ReelShort Dramas

Evan Adams stars in 5 short dramas on ReelShort, all in the teen/campus romance space:

- **The Senator's Son** (97M+ views) — plays Zach Walker, a senator's son who bullies-turned-loves classmate Emma Andrews
- **Kissed by Claw and Fang** (90M+ views) — supernatural love triangle between a werewolf heir and a vampire prince
- **Summer Situationship** (70M+ views) — plays Noah Allen, his ReelShort breakout role, opposite Jules Whitney
- **Oops! I'm in Love with My Stepbrother** (40M+ views) — forbidden stepsibling romance
- **The Virgin Camp Counselor** (35M+ views) — plays Asher Matthews, a summer-camp counselor

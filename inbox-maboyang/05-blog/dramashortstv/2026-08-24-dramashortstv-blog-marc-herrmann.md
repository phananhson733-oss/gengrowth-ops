## Before ReelShort: A Hallmark & Lifetime Movie Regular

Before short dramas, Marc Herrmann was already a familiar face in traditional TV movies — he's a recurring lead in Lifetime and Hallmark-style Christmas films, most often opposite his real-life wife, actress Anna Marie Dobbins. The two have appeared together in multiple holiday movies, including *Christmas at Fields Ranch* (2025), and are a well-known real-life couple in that circuit. He also had an early recognized role in the crime thriller series *Body in the Attic*.

That television-movie background is part of what makes his ReelShort performances stand out — he brings a soap-opera-trained delivery to short-form vertical drama, which leans hard on exactly that kind of heightened, expressive acting.

## From cover model to vertical drama lead

Before he was Elijah Baran, Eric Guilmette had already built a career on the covers of more than 200 romance novels — titles like *Liars Like Us* by J.T. Geissinger and *Blood Day* by Lexi C. Foss carry his face before you ever read a word inside. That's a specific kind of recognition: readers know the look before they know the name.

The shift into vertical drama built on that same audience. Guilmette has now led over 50 short-form series across nearly every major platform in the space — ReelShort, Pocket FM, Vigloo, ShortMax, DreameShort, DramaBox, and more. It's a genre that rewards a very particular skill: making a viewer feel something in a 90-second episode, over and over, fast enough to keep them tapping "next."

## The role that keeps coming up: playing trapped, powerful men

Elijah Baran isn't Guilmette's only genie-adjacent role, but it might be his most fitting one. The character spends most of [the series](https://dramashortstv.com/detail/37365/the-great-and-powerful-genie) absorbing disrespect he could end instantly if he chose to — and that restraint, played straight rather than campy, is exactly the register Guilmette has built a following on.

Other credits that show the range: *You Belong With Me* and *Love & Blood* on ReelShort (the latter he also produces), *Chosen By Fate* on Pocket FM, and *Campus Rivals* on Vigloo. Outside the vertical space, he appeared in the feature film *Tails of Christmas*, which premiered on Great American Family and Amazon Prime in December 2024.

## Recognition in the vertical drama industry

The awards aren't incidental. Guilmette was voted 2024 Vertical Legend and Best Actor In A Drama at the Vertical Drama Love Fan Awards, then won the Sapphire Vision Award at the Vertical Awards in September 2025. In a genre built almost entirely on audience retention, those wins reflect real, measurable viewer loyalty — not just industry buzz.

## He also writes

Guilmette's debut poetry collection, *sex and oatmeal*, released in August 2025 and reached #1 on Amazon's New Releases chart for Poetry — with the audiobook and Kindle editions holding the top spot for several weeks. It's an unusual second act for a face best known from vertical drama thumbnails, and it's part of why his fanbase follows him beyond any single role.

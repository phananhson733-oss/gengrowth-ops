## What Happens in You Are My Destiny

Giselle West — Gigi to the people who know her — is the woman who stood by Logan Hale through the worst stretch of his life and ultimately gave him her own corneas so he could see again. She disappears the moment he regains his sight, and Logan never learns she was carrying his child.

Five years pass. Logan returns as heir to the Hale family, still unaware that his wife and their ailing five-year-old daughter, Astra, are the very people he's been searching for since Giselle vanished. Regina Brown, a family friend played by Sarah Hamilton, works to tear the marriage apart before the truth can surface. ## Why the Blindness Twist Works

Vertical dramas hide identity behind secret royal bloodlines and disguised billionaires more often than not. You Are My Destiny does something more specific: Logan fell for Giselle while he couldn't physically see her, so when his sight finally returns, he doesn't have a face to search for. The thing that made their bond possible is the same thing that keeps him from recognizing her later.

That's a different engine than amnesia, which erases a memory that already existed. Here, the memory was never visual to begin with. It also explains why a Navy-pilot-turned-heir premise reads differently from the CEO and mafia leads that fill most of this catalog — Logan's power is inherited, but his vulnerability came first, and it came from a wound he didn't choose.

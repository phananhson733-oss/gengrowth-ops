# I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You: What the Historical Romance Is About

*Meta description: **I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You** follows Evelyn Arden, a betrayed woman who swaps weddings and marries a cursed prince in this historical romance.*

**I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You is a historical romance about Evelyn Arden choosing a cursed prince after Duke Lucien Storm betrays her for her sister.** The story starts with a wedding swap, then asks whether Lucien can earn back the woman he treated as a substitute.

For the official title details, visit [*I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You*](https://dramashortstv.com/detail/49020/i-d-rather-marry-the-beast-than-you).

## What Happens in *I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You*?

Evelyn Arden has loved Duke Lucien Storm for three years. What she does not know is that Lucien has been using her as a shield for her sister Vivian, the woman he truly loves.

Once Evelyn learns the truth, the relationship she thought she had is impossible to return to unchanged. Betrayed and heartbroken, she makes a public choice that gives the drama its central turn: she swaps weddings and marries the Empire's cursed monster prince.

Lucien only understands what he has lost after Evelyn stops waiting for him. He begins chasing her, but Evelyn's life is now moving in a different direction.

## Why Evelyn's Wedding Swap Matters

The wedding swap is more than a plot twist. It is Evelyn's answer to years of being treated as someone else's replacement.

Her marriage to the prince gives her a chance to step outside Lucien and Vivian's relationship. It also creates the drama's main romantic question: is the prince really the monster his reputation suggests, or is Evelyn meeting the first man who will see her as herself?

Lucien's late pursuit adds another layer. He wants Evelyn back only after losing the certainty that she would always be there. The story leaves viewers to decide whether regret is enough when trust has already been broken.

## Which Romance Tropes Does the Drama Use?

The official title page tags this drama as Romance, Historical, and Underdog Victory. Its premise also includes several familiar romance dynamics:

- **Betrayed heroine:** Evelyn discovers that Lucien's love was never honest.
- **Wedding swap:** A single decision changes her future in public.
- **Regretful former love:** Lucien chases Evelyn after realizing his mistake.
- **Feared new husband:** The cursed prince's reputation invites a closer look at who he really is.
- **Underdog victory:** Evelyn moves from being used as a shield to making her own choice.

## Who Is This Drama For?

Choose this title if you enjoy historical romances where the heroine does not quietly accept betrayal. The tension comes from Evelyn's decision to leave, not from waiting for Lucien to make up his mind.

It may also suit viewers who like a love triangle with a clear emotional cost: Lucien must face what he did, while Evelyn must decide whether a new relationship can offer the respect the old one lacked.

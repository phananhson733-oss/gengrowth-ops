## 四、正文（英文，可直接粘贴）

DramaBox is one of the larger short-drama apps, and most of what gets written about it repeats the same few claims. We went and checked the parts that are checkable.

### Who owns DramaBox

The Google Play listing names the developer as **STORYMATRIX PTE. LTD.**, registered at 250 North Bridge Road #29-03, Raffles City Tower, Singapore 179101, with a listed contact number of +65 8067 8178. The app's package identifier is `com.storymatrix.drama`, which matches.

This is worth stating plainly because a number of articles name a Beijing-based company as the owner instead. That name does not appear anywhere on the store listing. Google requires developers to publish verified trader details under its "About the developer" section, so the Singapore entity is the one on record.

Support is handled through feedback@dramabox.com, with gostoryofficial@gmail.com listed as the developer contact.

Whether STORYMATRIX has parent companies or investors behind it is a separate question, and not one the store listing answers.

### What DramaBox actually is

It is a vertical short-drama streaming app. Episodes run one to three minutes, series run anywhere from forty to a hundred episodes, and the format is built for phone viewing rather than television.

The catalog is mostly romance, revenge, werewolf and CEO storylines, produced fast and specifically for this format. It is not a licensing platform for existing shows.

The first handful of episodes in a series are usually free. After that you either unlock episodes individually with coins or subscribe.

### How much DramaBox costs

The Play Store lists in-app purchases from **$0.49 to $299.99 per item**. That range is the honest answer, and it is wide because it covers both small coin top-ups and large bundles.

The figure you will see quoted elsewhere is a weekly subscription somewhere between seventeen and twenty dollars. We could not confirm that number from a first-party source, and prices for these apps vary by region and by promotion, so treat any single figure you read as a starting point rather than a quote.

What matters more than the headline price is the structure. Coin unlocking and subscription billing are separate systems, and buying coins does not stop a subscription from renewing. People get caught by this.

### Is DramaBox legit

In the sense most people mean when they ask: yes. It is a real app from a registered company, distributed through the official Apple and Google stores, with published trader details and a working support address. It is not a scam app.

The complaints that come up repeatedly are about billing rather than legitimacy. Weekly renewal is easy to start and easy to forget, the app keeps working after you cancel until the period ends, and refunds go through Apple or Google rather than the developer.

That distinction is worth holding onto. An app can be entirely legitimate and still cost you more than you meant to spend.

### How to cancel a DramaBox subscription

The single most useful thing to know here: **deleting the app does not cancel anything.** Billing is held by Apple or Google, not by DramaBox, so it continues until you cancel at the platform level.

**On iPhone or iPad.** Open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Find DramaBox in the list, tap it, and choose Cancel Subscription. If it is not in the list, the subscription may be under a different Apple ID than the one currently signed in.

**On Android.** Open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Select DramaBox and tap Cancel subscription.

**If you subscribed on the web** rather than through an app store, cancel from within your DramaBox account settings instead, since neither Apple nor Google will have a record of it.

You keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for. If you were charged after cancelling, that is a refund request to Apple or Google, not to DramaBox.

### What the reviews actually say

Store ratings for these apps tend to be high while the written reviews skew negative, and the gap is not a contradiction. People who enjoy the content rate and leave. People who get surprised by a charge write paragraphs.

The recurring themes are the weekly billing cadence, the speed at which coins get consumed, and difficulty finding where to cancel. Content quality complaints exist but are a distant second.

If you are deciding whether to install it, the reviews are more useful as a warning about the payment model than as a verdict on the app.

### The short version

DramaBox is a legitimate app from STORYMATRIX PTE. LTD. in Singapore. In-app purchases run from $0.49 to $299.99 per item. Cancellation happens in your Apple or Google account, not in the app, and uninstalling does nothing to stop billing.

If you want to try the format without committing to a subscription, most of these apps including this one give you the first several episodes of any series for free.

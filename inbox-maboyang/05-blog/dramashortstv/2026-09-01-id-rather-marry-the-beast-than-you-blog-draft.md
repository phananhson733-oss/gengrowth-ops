## What Is *I'd Rather Marry the Beast Than You* About?

Evelyn Arden has spent years loving Lucien Storm, a duke who never tells her the whole truth. Behind their relationship is a painful betrayal: Lucien sees Evelyn as protection for her sister Vivian, the woman he truly loves.

When that betrayal leaves Evelyn tortured and heartbroken, she refuses to remain in the role someone else assigned to her. She swaps weddings and marries the Empire's cursed monster prince.

That decision turns a familiar love triangle into something sharper. Lucien only understands what he has lost when Evelyn is no longer waiting for him. The question is no longer whether he loves her. It is whether he has realized it too late.

## Why Evelyn Arden's Choice Changes the Historical Romance

At its core, this is a second-chance romance story, but Evelyn is not simply asked to forgive and return to the life she had before. Her decision to marry another man gives the story its emotional stakes.

For Evelyn, the wedding swap is a break from humiliation and from the expectation that she should accept being treated as a substitute. For Lucien, it is the moment when regret becomes unavoidable.

The setup also makes room for a new relationship. The cursed prince is introduced as the Empire's “monster prince,” a label that invites readers to look past reputation and ask who he really is when Evelyn meets him on her own terms.

Module · FAQ (8–10 items)

Questions

Is this really free?
Yes — full scans are free. Paid tiers only cover ongoing costs like repeated automated re-checks.
Does it work without Search Console?
No — this tool reads your own site's private search performance data, which only Search Console has. That's different from tools like our Free SEO Audit, which work on any public URL with no login.
What data does it need?
Read-only Search Console access. We never request permission to change anything on your site.
What counts as "high impressions, low CTR"?
Position 4–20, impressions above your site average, and CTR well under what's typical for that position range.
What's a "near breakthrough" page?
Position 11–20 with impressions trending up — close enough to page one that a small push can matter.
Can I disconnect Search Console later?
Yes, anytime, from your account settings.
Does this replace Ahrefs or Semrush?
No — it's not a general audit tool. It only looks at pages Google is already showing people.
What happens after I fix a page?
It stays in your project so you can track whether the CTR actually improved.

Why Is My Organic Traffic Dropping?

GenGrowth Team · Jul 24, 2026 · 7 min read · 👁 1,204 reads

Module · Article body (rich text, ~45% mid-CTA — now the primary conversion point in this article, promoted from the top card per the reasoning above)

"Traffic dropped" is doing a lot of work. It usually hides at least three different problems — a rankings drop, a clicks drop your rankings didn't cause, and normal seasonal noise you mistook for a trend. Before touching a single page, the first job is naming which one you're actually looking at.

Step 1 — Confirm it's not seasonal or a demand shift

Compare the same weeks year-over-year, not just the last 30 days against the 30 before. If a topic has predictable seasonal demand, a dip that looks alarming in isolation can be completely normal once you line it up against last year's curve.

Step 2 — Separate a rankings drop from a clicks drop

Pull impressions and average position for the same query set across both periods. If position held steady but clicks fell, you're not looking at a rankings problem at all — you're looking at a click-through problem, and no amount of new content will fix a title tag that stopped matching what people expected to see.

This is the exact pattern SEO Quick Wins is built to catch: pages still ranking fine, quietly losing clicks anyway.

Want your own impressions-vs-clicks breakdown?

Connect Search Console and see which of your pages have this exact pattern. Free · No credit card · Takes about 2 minutes

Step 3 — Check whether Google can still reach the page

Sometimes a page didn't get worse — it got cut off. A site restructure, a broken internal link, or a page that quietly became an orphan (no other page on the site links to it) can starve a page of the crawl attention and internal authority it used to get, without anything on the page itself changing.

The pattern worth knowing

Most "traffic dropped" investigations end up being one of these three things, and each has a completely different fix. Diagnose before you touch anything — rewriting content to fix a broken internal link wastes a week and changes nothing.

**这页不做关键词优化，但不等于要写短（2026-07-30修正）**

初版把这页写成880词、理由是"品类打不动所以能删就删"——那个判断只从SEO角度出发，**漏掉了它的转化角色**。品类KD 76-94确实打不动，硬堆关键词没有意义，这部分结论不变。但它同时是**主页输入框的承接页**，很多用户对GenGrowth产品深度的第一印象就落在这一页，写太薄反而传递"这产品也就这样"。

**本页有一块只有它能讲的内容**：P0-4是唯一一个触及全部其他工具的入口——它扫出的meta、标题结构、断链、孤岛、索引状态、CWV、schema，每一类都对应一个更深的工具或四步工作流里的某一步。**它是整个系统的枢纽**，这个故事放在别的页面讲不通，只有这里讲得通。这比凑字数有价值。

修订后约1,400词。写作标准是：**不为SEO加长，为"讲清楚整个系统"加长**。

### ⚠️ 前端必须先确认：本页有两种状态

| 状态 | 触发 | 文案的作用 |
|---|---|---|
| **带结果** | 从主页输入框跑完审计，点"查看完整审计"进来 | 用户要的是结果。结果区置顶，营销文案应下移或折叠 |
| **冷启动** | 从导航/footer/内链直接进来，没跑过 | 用户先看到文案，这时篇幅和深度才起作用 |

**两种状态用同一份文案、但排序不同**，需要前端确认实现方式。这也是本页跟其余四页最大的结构差异——其余四页只有冷启动一种状态。

## [专属] 枢纽说明 —— 每一类发现去哪里（不进编号体系）

```
[H2] Where each finding goes next

[说明]
An audit that ends in a list is a to-do list you'll ignore. Every category this
scan checks hands off somewhere — to a tool that goes deeper, or to a step in your
project. Here's the map.

[H3] Titles and meta → SEO Quick Wins
If a page has a title problem and is already getting impressions, that's not a
technical issue — it's clicks you're losing right now. SEO Quick Wins ranks those
by how much traffic is recoverable.

[H3] Broken links and orphan pages → Internal Link Audit
This scan tells you how many there are. The Internal Link Audit tells you which
pages should link to them, ranked by how much authority each source can pass.

[H3] Indexability problems → fix before anything else
A page blocked by robots.txt or carrying a stray noindex can't rank no matter what
else you improve. These sit at the top of the list for a reason.

[H3] Core Web Vitals → measure before you rebuild
Slow pages hurt, but rebuilding a site for a metric that wasn't your bottleneck is
an expensive mistake. We show the number; whether it's your constraint depends on
what else is on the list.

[H3] Structured data → the AI-search side of the same job
Schema is how you tell search engines and AI assistants what a page is. It's
increasingly what decides whether you get cited in an AI answer, not just ranked.

[H3] Everything → Step 1 of your project
The full finding set becomes the starting position for a GenGrowth project, so the
four steps that follow are working from your actual state rather than a blank page.
```

## [专属] 怎么读这个评分（不进编号体系）

```
[H2] How to read your score

[H3] The letter is for tracking you, not comparing you
A C+ on a 40-page site and a C+ on a 4,000-page site describe completely different
situations. The score is useful for watching your own number move over time. It
means nothing across sites.

[H3] Read the list, not the grade
Two sites with identical scores can have entirely different problems — one with
many small issues, one with a few severe ones. The ranked issue list is the
actual output; the score is a summary of it.

[H3] Start at the top and stop when it gets expensive
Issues are ordered by likely impact against effort. Somewhere down the list the
work stops being worth it for your situation. That line is yours to draw, and it's
usually higher than people expect.
```

## [区块10] 限制说明（**默认折叠**）

```
[H2] What a free audit can't do          [折叠 · 3 known limits]

[H3] It isn't a full-site crawl
The free audit covers your most important pages, not every URL. Large sites will
get a representative sample, not a complete inventory.

[H3] It can't see JavaScript-injected content
We don't execute long-running client-side JavaScript. On heavily client-rendered
sites, some links and content will be invisible to the audit.

[H3] A score is a starting point, not a verdict
Two sites with the same score can have completely different problems. Read the
issue list, not the letter grade.
```

## [区块11] FAQ

```
[H2] Free SEO audit FAQ

[H3] Is this SEO audit really free?
Yes. No account, no card, no trial that expires. Paid tiers exist for repeated
automated re-checks and full-site crawls, not for seeing your results.

[H3] Do I need to verify my site or connect Search Console?
No. This reads publicly available pages, so there's nothing to verify and nothing
to authorize.

[H3] Can I audit a website I don't own?
Yes — that's a direct consequence of only reading public pages. Competitor
research, prospect qualification, and site takeovers are all common uses.

[H3] How many pages do you check?
Up to [X] per audit, starting from your homepage and sitemap. Larger sites get the
most important pages first.

[H3] How is this different from the Internal Link Audit?
This is broad and shallow across many SEO factors. The Internal Link Audit is
narrow and deep on one: how your pages connect to each other.

[H3] What does the score actually mean?
It's a weighted summary of the issues found, useful for tracking your own progress
over time. It's not comparable across sites, and a good score doesn't mean you'll
rank.

[H3] How is this different from Semrush or Ahrefs site audit?
Theirs are more thorough, crawl deeper, and cost money. This one is free, needs no
account, and returns in about twenty seconds. Different tools for different moments.

[H3] Can I export or share the report?
Yes, as CSV. Findings also carry into your GenGrowth project if you continue.

[H3] How often should I run it?
After any significant change — a migration, a redesign, a template update — and
quarterly otherwise. Running it weekly on a static site mostly produces noise.

[H3] Do you store my URL or the report?
Audit results are cached briefly so repeat requests for the same domain return
faster. [具体保留时长待工程/法务确认后填入]

[H3] Do I need an account to see my results?
No. The audit runs and displays without one. An account only matters if you want to
save the report, re-run it on a schedule, or carry the findings into a project.

[H3] What should I fix first?
Start with anything under indexability — a page that can't be indexed can't rank,
so nothing else you do to it matters. After that, work down the ranked list until
the effort stops being worth it for your situation.
```
